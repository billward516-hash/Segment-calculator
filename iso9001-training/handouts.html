Audit Response Card

Meridian Group pocket card. Clause reference: 9.2 and Module 10.

This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.

What an auditor does

An auditor gathers evidence by interview, observation, and record review. Auditors sample. Evidence counts more than statements.

How to respond

  1. Answer the question asked. Keep the answer short.
  2. Be truthful. Never guess or invent an answer.
  3. Say "I will find out" when unsure. Then tell your supervisor.
  4. Show the record or the instruction. Evidence beats statements.
  5. Do not volunteer unrelated information.
  6. Do not argue. If you disagree, state your point calmly and let your supervisor follow up.
  7. Refer questions about another area to your supervisor.

Be ready to show

  • The quality policy, and what it means for your work.
  • The objectives your station influences.
  • Where controlled documents are, and how you know they are current.
  • How you hold nonconforming items (STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT).
  • Your training record and qualification status.
  • The calibration or verification status of the instruments you use.
  • Records completed at the time of the activity.

Before the audit

  • Keep your area tidy and your records current.
  • Remove uncontrolled copies and personal notes.
  • Know your authority to stop a process.

Audit terms

TermMeaning
FindingA statement of what was found, compared with the criteria
NonconformityA requirement not met. Some bodies grade them as major or minor
ObservationA point worth improving that is not a nonconformity

Confirm the terms used with the certification body.

STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT

Meridian Group pocket card. Clause reference: 8.7.

This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.

When to use this card

Use it when you find or suspect that a work item, material, or output does not meet its requirements. This includes a result outside its limit, a damaged or mislabeled item, a missing record, or an overdue measuring resource.

The four steps

StepAction
1. STOPStop the process if the cause is still active. Do not continue work that could produce more nonconforming output.
2. FLAGMark the work item and any affected material. Use a physical tag or an electronic status. State what is wrong, the date, and your name.
3. HOLDMove it to the hold area or set the hold status so that it cannot proceed or be delivered.
4. REPORTTell your supervisor and quality immediately. Give the work item identification, what you found, and what you did.

Do not

  • Redo, rework, or scrap without a documented disposition.
  • Release "just this once".
  • Leave suspect material at the station.
  • Wait to see whether the problem repeats.
  • Change a record to hide the problem.

If the item has already left

Report it at once. Quality and the supervisor decide whether the customer must be informed and what further action is needed.

Remember

  • Speed of containment protects the customer.
  • Reporting is expected and valued at Meridian Group.
  • Only an authorized person decides what happens to a held item.
