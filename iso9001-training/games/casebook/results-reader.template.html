Training aid Results codes summarize a learner's case attempt: the case code, the five measures, the decision result, the issues found, and the date. They are a training aid, not an assessment record, and they are not tamper-proof. The Sector Practitioner badge requires an instructor to review the learner's capstone findings and, preferably, to observe a short oral or on-the-job demonstration. No measure decides the badge on its own.

No accounts are used and nothing is sent anywhere. Codes stay on this device unless you save or print them.

Codes from the optional placement check (PLC-P-…) are listed in their own table with the pass rule applied. Codes from 2026 variant cases are marked and shown under the 2026 caution; they are not counted toward the badge until the 2026 content is verified.

This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.
