Sector Showdown card deck

Players: teams of 3 to 5. Time: 20 to 30 minutes. Preparation: print the pages for one sector on card stock and cut along the dashed lines. Each row holds a question card, its answer card, and its clause card.

  1. The facilitator reads a question card aloud. Every team answers the same question on paper or a mini whiteboard: a letter and, if it can, a clause number.
  2. Teams reveal together. Turn over the answer card. One point for each team with the correct letter.
  3. Show the clause card. A team that cited the correct clause earns one bonus point. Any clause listed on the card counts.
  4. After 12 questions, total the points and run the debrief.

Variants

  • Cross-Sector Swap: give each team another sector's deck.
  • Streak Round: consecutive correct answers score 1, 2, 3 and so on; a wrong answer resets the streak.
  • Exit Ticket: draw five question cards at random at the end of a session.

Debrief prompts

  • Which question looked sector-specific but tested a general requirement?
  • Which clause appeared most often?
  • What would you do differently at your workstation?
