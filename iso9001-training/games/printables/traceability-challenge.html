Traceability Challenge: facilitator sheet

Meridian Group (Generic (sector-neutral)). Seed 12345. Teams of 3 to 5. 30 to 40 minutes. Modules 6 and 7 (Clauses 8.5.2 and 8.7).

Materials per team

  • The week of records: work items register, input batch issue log, and equipment and system log.
  • One complaint card (issue at minute 5).
  • One containment worksheet.

Timing

MinuteStep
0 to 5Teams read the records. Explain that the records are all the information they will have.
5Issue the complaint card. Start the clock.
5 to 10Backward trace: what did the failing work item pass through?
10 to 25Forward trace: which other work items used the suspect input batch, and where are they now?
25Teams hand in the containment worksheet. Record the finishing time.
25 to 40Score against the key and debrief.

Rule given to teams

A work item may be left out of the containment list only if a record shows that it used a different input batch. If the records cannot show which input batch was used, the work item must be contained.

Scoring

CriterionPoints
Each work item on the key that is on the team list+2
Each work item on the team list that is not on the key (unnecessary recall)−1
Correct action for each listed work item (customer notification and recall, or hold in house)+1
Backward trace complete (date, shift, line, input batch, people, delivery note)+3
Speed: first, second, and third correct list+3, +2, +1

Facilitator key: notes

What the record gaps cost

2 work items on the list (W-121, W-126) did not use the suspect input batch. The facilitator knows this; the teams cannot, because the batch field is blank and the issue log has no entry for Unit 1 from Wed B to Thu B (the missing entry was IB-2472). These are unnecessary recalls caused by record gaps. Blank batch fields that the issue log resolves to another input batch (W-131, W-139) may be excluded; a team that contains them loses 1 point each.

Red herring: the planned maintenance on Unit 2 (Wed A) does not affect the containment list. The suspect input batch was split between both lines, so a team that traces only Unit 2 will miss part of the list.

Traceability Challenge: debrief

Debrief

QuestionExpected answer
Which record gaps expanded the recall?The blank input batch fields in the register and the missing issue log entry. Without them the team cannot prove that a work item used a different input batch, so it must be contained.
What does this imply for the floor?Complete the identification fields at the time of the activity, every time (Clauses 8.5.2 and 7.5). A blank field costs far more later than it saves now.
Why did some teams miss part of the list?They traced only the line on the complaint record. The suspect input batch was split between two lines. Forward tracing must follow the input, not the line.
What is the difference between the backward and the forward trace?Backward: from the failing work item to its inputs, equipment, people, and records. Forward: from the suspect input to every work item that used it and where each one is now.
Who decides to notify the customer, and when?The person with that authority under the company procedure, without delay once delivered work items may be affected [OWNER INPUT]. Clause 8.7 covers the control of nonconforming outputs, including those detected after delivery.
Which requirements does this exercise relate to?Clause 8.5.2 (identification and traceability), 8.7 (nonconforming outputs), 7.5 (records), and 10.2 (corrective action for the cause).
