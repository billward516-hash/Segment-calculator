Circle one letter for each question.

  1. A technician finds that the process instruction at the station is an earlier revision. What should be done?
    • A. Ask the previous shift.
    • B. Continue and make a note.
    • C. Stop, obtain the current revision, and notify the supervisor.
    • D. Work from memory.
  2. A thickness gauge shows an expired calibration date. What is correct?
    • A. Keep using it and add notes.
    • B. Do not use it; flag it and report it.
    • C. Pass the lot using visual checks only.
    • D. Use it with a margin.
  3. A particle count above the limit is recorded on a lot already staged for packing. What is the first action?
    • A. Re-measure until it passes.
    • B. Pack it and flag it later.
    • C. Move it to another cart.
    • D. Stop the movement, flag and hold the lot, and notify the supervisor and quality.
  4. Wafers supplied by a customer for processing are best described as:
    • A. Scrap.
    • B. Customer property to be identified, protected, and reported if lost or damaged.
    • C. The organization's inventory.
    • D. A consumable.
  5. A lot is split and recombined without a record. Which requirement is most directly compromised?
    • A. Policy.
    • B. Infrastructure planning.
    • C. Traceability.
    • D. Context.
  6. A cleanroom particle monitor alarms during a run. Which clause area addresses the conditions needed for the process?
    • A. Quality objectives.
    • B. Environment for the operation of processes.
    • C. Context of the organization.
    • D. Customer property.
  7. A chemical lot arrives without its certificate of conformance. What is appropriate?
    • A. Use it on non-critical lots first.
    • B. Ask the operator to judge.
    • C. Use it with extra rinsing.
    • D. Hold it and verify it before use.
  8. Engineering adjusts a polish pressure recipe. What is required before use?
    • A. Verbal approval.
    • B. Operators try it on one lot.
    • C. Review of consequences, authorization, communication, and records.
    • D. A note in the shift log.
  9. A recurring scratch defect has been scrapped each time. What is missing?
    • A. A new label.
    • B. More scrapping.
    • C. Cause investigation, action to eliminate the cause, and an effectiveness check.
    • D. Nothing.
  10. Operators record gauge readings at the end of shift from memory. What is the main risk?
    • A. Higher speed.
    • B. Lower cost.
    • C. Records that cannot serve as reliable objective evidence.
    • D. Fewer records.
  11. A lot with an open discrepancy is proposed for release to meet a shipment date. What is the correct response?
    • A. Release it at the supervisor's request without records.
    • B. Hold it until resolved or until release is authorized by a person with the authority (and the customer where applicable).
    • C. Release it; the schedule matters.
    • D. Ship half.
  12. Why are training records retained?
    • A. To reduce audit time.
    • B. For human resources only.
    • C. To satisfy equipment vendors.
    • D. As evidence of competence.

Circle one letter for each question.

  1. Meridian Microworks starts offering a wafer reclaim service. What must be checked about its quality management system scope?
    • A. Only the website wording.
    • B. Nothing; the scope never changes.
    • C. Only the price list.
    • D. Whether the scope covers the new service, and whether any requirement claimed as not applicable is still justified.
  2. Managers approve quality objectives but never provide resources for the planned actions. What does this show?
    • A. A customer property problem.
    • B. Strong leadership.
    • C. A calibration problem.
    • D. Weak leadership commitment; top management is expected to make resources available so the system achieves its intended results.
  3. A new operator does not know why the particle limit matters to the customer. Which requirement area applies?
    • A. Infrastructure.
    • B. Design and development.
    • C. External provider evaluation.
    • D. Awareness.
  4. Which is a well-formed quality objective for the fab?
    • A. "Better yield."
    • B. "Reduce customer-reported particle defects below an agreed rate per thousand lots by year end, reviewed monthly."
    • C. "No problems."
    • D. "Try harder."
  5. A risk review of final clean ranks one failure mode as high. What should follow?
    • A. Plan actions proportionate to the risk, build them into the process, and evaluate their effectiveness.
    • B. File the review.
    • C. Lower the ranking.
    • D. Wait for a customer complaint.
  6. A wet bench is repeatedly used past its scheduled maintenance date. Which requirement is most relevant?
    • A. Maintenance of the infrastructure needed for conforming product.
    • B. Customer property.
    • C. Organizational context.
    • D. Quality policy.
  7. An engineering hold on a recipe is announced only by email to day shift. Night shift runs the recipe. What is the gap?
    • A. Internal communication that reaches everyone affected.
    • B. Calibration.
    • C. Customer focus.
    • D. Design and development.
  8. A customer asks for a new film stack the fab has never produced. Which requirement area governs planning, reviews, verification, and validation of the new process?
    • A. Design and development.
    • B. Purchasing only.
    • C. Customer property.
    • D. Management review only.
  9. Lots wait in open carriers outside the cleanroom during a tool outage. Which requirement is most at risk?
    • A. Preservation of outputs.
    • B. Organizational context.
    • C. Internal audit.
    • D. Quality policy.
  10. A customer telephones a technician with a complaint. What should happen?
    • A. Tell the customer to email someone else.
    • B. Record it and pass it into the customer communication and complaint process.
    • C. Forget it after the call.
    • D. Handle it informally.
  11. Yield data shows a slow downward trend that has not yet breached any limit. What is expected?
    • A. Analyze and evaluate the trend and decide whether action is needed.
    • B. Stop collecting the data.
    • C. Change the limit.
    • D. Wait for a breach.
  12. Which statement describes improvement in the quality management system?
    • A. Only fixing defects.
    • B. Only customer audits.
    • C. Selecting and acting on opportunities to improve products, services, processes, and results, including correcting and preventing problems.
    • D. Only buying new tools.
