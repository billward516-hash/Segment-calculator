Audit Day: Meridian Group

Pack: Generic (sector-neutral). Audit date in the dossier: 14 Oct 2026. Dossier seed: 2026.

Roles

  • Lead auditor: plans the sample, leads questions, writes findings.
  • Auditors: gather evidence by interview and record review.
  • Auditees: answer using the dossier only; they may not invent evidence.
  • Observer: scores audit technique with the observer checklist.

Rounds

RoundMinutesTask
1. Planning10Choose three processes to sample. Assign the lead auditor, auditors, auditees, and observer. Prepare questions.
2. Evidence gathering25Interview the auditees and review the dossier records. Auditees answer from the dossier only and may not invent evidence.
3. Findings15Write each finding with the clause, the evidence, and the category: major, minor, or observation.
4. Closing meeting10Present findings. Auditees may ask clarifying questions. Do not argue the classification.
5. Corrective action planning15Auditees draft a response for each finding: correction, cause, corrective action, and effectiveness check.

Scoring

  • 1 point for each planted nonconformity found with the correct clause.
  • 1 bonus point for each of those findings that is properly worded (requirement, evidence, statement of what was not met).
  • Minus 1 point for each finding not supported by dossier evidence.
  • Observer checklist: 0 to 2 points on each of four criteria (maximum 8).

D1 Quality policy (QP-01)

Meridian Group delivers work items that meet what our customers have asked for and the rules that apply to our work.

We set measurable quality objectives each year, publish the results, and review them at management review.

Every employee may stop a process when a work item may not meet requirements. We report problems openly and without blame.

We improve our processes using data, audit results, and customer feedback. Managers provide the people, equipment, and time that quality work needs.
