    • A. Record only the cost.

    • A. Competence determined, training given, and evidence retained.

  11. Which is an appropriate measurable objective?
    • A. "Make customers happy."
    • B. "At least 99 percent of orders delivered complete and on time."
    • C. "Improve."
    • D. "Be fast."
  12. Why are internal audits performed?
    • A. To set prices.
    • B. To assign blame.
    • C. To replace management review.

  1. A major customer moves to next-day delivery for all orders. Where should Meridian Logistics first take account of this?
    • A. Nowhere.
    • B. Only at the next audit.
    • C. Only in the transport schedule.
    • D. In its review of external issues and interested-party requirements, and then in planning.
  2. A new returns process is introduced. What should be determined for it?
    • A. Only the cost.
    • B. Only the dock door number.
    • C. Its inputs, outputs, sequence, interactions, criteria, resources, responsibilities, and how its performance is monitored.
    • D. Nothing.
  3. Managers tell staff that quality matters but reward only speed. What does leadership commitment require?
    • A. Nothing more.
    • B. A focus on speed only.
    • C. Leaving quality to the quality team.
    • D. Promoting customer focus and making sure the system achieves its intended results, with consistent direction.
  4. A picker does not know that a wrong item leads to a customer complaint and a return. Which requirement area applies?
    • A. Customer property.
    • B. Awareness.
    • C. Calibration.
    • D. Design and development.
  5. Peak season will double volume. What should planning include?
    • A. Identifying risks to on-time and accurate delivery, planning proportionate actions, and evaluating them.
    • B. Cancelling orders.
    • C. Hoping volume can be absorbed.
    • D. Ignoring the forecast.
  6. Handheld scanners fail often, and staff key in codes by hand. What is the concern?
    • A. Costs fall.
    • B. Work becomes faster.
    • C. Infrastructure needed for conforming work is not maintained, which raises the chance of errors.
    • D. There is no concern.
  7. A customer changes a delivery address, but the change does not reach the loading team. What is the gap?
    • A. Calibration.
    • B. Organizational context.
    • C. Quality policy.
    • D. Communication of changes to the people who need them.
  8. Sales agrees to a delivery window the fleet cannot meet. What should have happened?
    • A. A review of the requirement and confirmation of capability before commitment.
    • B. Nothing.
    • C. Drivers should try harder.
    • D. The customer should adjust.
  9. Goods are stored under a leaking roof. Which requirement is most at risk?
    • A. Organizational context.
    • B. Internal audit.
    • C. Preservation of outputs.
    • D. Quality policy.
  10. A customer asks for collection of damaged goods under the service agreement. Which requirement area applies?
    • A. Quality policy.
    • B. Calibration.
    • C. Design and development.
    • D. Post-delivery activities.
  11. Mis-pick data is collected but never analyzed. What is missing?
    • A. Nothing.
    • B. More collection.
    • C. Analysis and evaluation to find causes and trends.
    • D. A new form.
  12. What should management review result in?
    • A. Only a date for the next meeting.
    • B. A list of attendees.
    • C. Decisions and actions on improvement, changes to the system, and resource needs.
    • D. Only meeting minutes.
