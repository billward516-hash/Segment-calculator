Circle one letter for each question.

  1. The label artwork on the packaging line is the previous revision. What should happen?
    • A. Stop the run, hold the product already labeled, and obtain the current approved artwork.
    • B. Finish the run, then correct.
    • C. Ask the line operator to decide.
    • D. Cover the difference with a sticker.
  2. A temperature probe used to verify processing has no verification record for the required interval. What is the proper response?
    • A. Adjust the limit.
    • B. Continue; the probe looked fine.
    • C. Ask another operator.
    • D. Stop using it, flag it, and assess the validity of earlier readings.
  3. A laboratory result needed for batch release is still pending at the end of shift. What should happen?
    • A. Release it and update later.
    • B. Release it if sales requests.
    • C. The batch remains on hold until the result is reviewed and release is authorized.
    • D. Release it on the supervisor's estimate.
  4. A customer supplies its own printed packaging. A pallet arrives with water damage. Which action is correct?
    • A. Use all of it; it is only packaging.
    • B. Identify and protect the packaging, record the damage, and inform the customer.
    • C. Return it without a record.
    • D. Use the dry boxes and discard the rest quietly.
  5. A customer complaint concerns one batch. Which capability limits the scope of a review or recall?
    • A. Marketing planning.
    • B. Management review minutes.
    • C. Batch identification and traceability to ingredient and packaging lots.
    • D. Staff awareness.
  6. An ingredient arrives past the date by which the supplier states it should be used. What is the best action?
    • A. Use it first because it is aging.
    • B. Accept it if the driver agrees.
    • C. Do not use it; hold it, identify it, and follow the nonconformance procedure.
    • D. Mix it with newer stock.
  7. A process parameter is changed to improve yield. What is required?
    • A. Review of consequences, authorization, communication, and records.
    • B. Only the supervisor needs to know.
    • C. Verbal approval from any operator.
    • D. An informal note on the shift sheet.
  8. A new operator has not been qualified on the filling step. What does the organization need before independent work?
    • A. Allow it if the shift is busy.
    • B. Allow it if a colleague is nearby.
    • C. Allow it with no record.
    • D. Competence determined, training provided, and evidence retained.
  9. The same labeling error recurs monthly. Retraining operators each time has not solved it. What should be done?
    • A. Retrain more often.
    • B. Find the underlying cause in the system (instruction, tooling, workload, checks) and act on it.
    • C. Add a warning sign only.
    • D. Remove the line.
  10. Which input does management review normally consider?
    • A. Staff lunch preferences.
    • B. Competitor pricing only.
    • C. Customer feedback, process performance, nonconformities, audit results, and provider performance.
    • D. Weather forecasts only.
  11. A supervisor proposes shipping a batch with an unresolved labeling discrepancy because the truck is waiting. What is the right response?
    • A. Ship half.
    • B. Hold the batch until the discrepancy is resolved or release is authorized as required.
    • C. Ship it; the truck is waiting.
    • D. Relabel on the truck without a record.
  12. Quality objectives should be:
    • A. Kept secret.
    • B. Set by the auditor.
    • C. Fixed forever.
    • D. Measurable, consistent with the policy, monitored, and communicated.

Circle one letter for each question.

  1. A retail customer announces that it will require recyclable packaging from next year. Where should Meridian Foods first take account of this?
    • A. Only on the label.
    • B. Nowhere; retailer plans are not relevant.
    • C. In its review of external issues and interested-party requirements that affect its system and planning.
    • D. Only at the next audit.
  2. On-time delivery has fallen for several months, but no customer has complained formally. What does customer focus expect of leadership?
    • A. Reduce monitoring.
    • B. Make sure customer requirements are determined and met, and act on risks to customer satisfaction.
    • C. Wait for a formal complaint.
    • D. Blame the carrier.
  3. An operator is asked what the quality policy means for the packaging line. Which answer shows awareness?
    • A. "I do not need to know it."
    • B. "It commits us to meeting customer requirements, so I check labels and codes against the approved specification."
    • C. "Only managers use it."
    • D. "It is on the wall in the office."
  4. A long-serving technician knows a changeover sequence that is not written down anywhere. What should the organization do?
    • A. Capture the knowledge so it is maintained and available, for example in an instruction or in training.
    • B. Nothing; the changeover works.
    • C. Ask the technician never to leave.
    • D. Keep it confidential to the technician.
  5. The only filling machine has no backup and has failed twice this quarter. What is a proportionate planning response?
    • A. Wait for the next failure.
    • B. Ask customers to order less.
    • C. Buy three new machines immediately.
    • D. Assess the risk to deliveries and conformity, plan actions such as spare parts or a revised maintenance plan, and review whether they worked.
  6. The lighting in the packing area is too dim to read batch codes reliably. Which requirement area is most relevant?
    • A. Environment for the operation of processes.
    • B. Customer property.
    • C. Quality policy.
    • D. Management review.
  7. An entry on a batch record has been covered with correction fluid, with no initials or date. What is the concern?
    • A. Appearance only.
    • B. The cost of paper.
    • C. There is no concern.
    • D. Changes to the record are not controlled, so it may not be trustworthy evidence.
  8. A new customer asks for a different pack size and case configuration. Before the order is accepted, what should be confirmed?
    • A. Nothing; the line can adapt.
    • B. That the requirements, including any the customer did not state but that are needed, are determined and can be met.
    • C. Only the price.
    • D. That marketing likes the design.
  9. The reject bin on the checkweigher is unlocked, and rejected packs can be returned to the line. What is the concern?
    • A. Controls meant to keep rejected output out of the product flow can be bypassed.
    • B. There is no concern.
    • C. Costs are lower.
    • D. The line runs faster.
  10. After delivery, a customer asks for support with a question about the packaging of a batch. Which requirement area covers this?
    • A. Organizational context.
    • B. Calibration.
    • C. Post-delivery activities.
    • D. Internal audit.
  11. The plant tracks many indicators but has not decided which ones show whether processes achieve their planned results. What is missing?
    • A. A decision on what to monitor and measure, how, when, and when the results are analyzed.
    • B. More dashboards.
    • C. A new software tool.
    • D. Fewer customers.
  12. An internal auditor asks an operator to show how she checks label codes. What is the best response?
    • A. Say what she thinks the auditor wants to hear.
    • B. Refuse to answer.
    • C. Send the auditor to the supervisor without answering.
    • D. Show the actual practice and the record, and say so honestly if unsure.
