Circle one letter for each question.

  1. A crew is building to a drawing that is not the current revision. What should the crew leader do?
    • A. Stop the affected work, obtain the current drawing, and assess the work already done.
    • B. Complete the work package first.
    • C. Ask the designer informally.
    • D. Continue and fix it later.
  2. Survey instruments used to set levels are past their verification date. What is correct?
    • A. Stop using them, flag them, and assess earlier readings.
    • B. Use them only for minor measurements.
    • C. Remove the label.
    • D. Continue.
  3. A hold point requires inspection sign-off before concrete is poured. The inspector is delayed. What should happen?
    • A. The foreman signs for the inspector.
    • B. Pour and inspect later.
    • C. Pour half.
    • D. Wait for the inspection or an authorized release.
  4. A client supplies existing drawings and site access for the work. How should these be treated?
    • A. As disposable.
    • B. As confidential only if requested.
    • C. As the contractor's property.
    • D. As customer property that must be identified, protected, and reported if lost or damaged.
  5. A subcontractor starts work without being evaluated. What is the issue?
    • A. There is no issue.
    • B. It concerns training only.
    • C. Externally provided processes must be controlled and providers evaluated.
    • D. It concerns site safety only.
  6. A change of concrete supplier is proposed mid-project. What is appropriate?
    • A. Review the effect, approve, communicate, verify the new supply, and keep records.
    • B. Ask the crew.
    • C. Switch quietly.
    • D. Switch only if it is cheaper.
  7. Concrete test results are not recorded for one pour. What is the main concern?
    • A. Appearance.
    • B. Wasted paper.
    • C. Lack of evidence of conformity and of who authorized release.
    • D. Weather.
  8. A misplaced anchor bolt is corrected. Which statement distinguishes correction from corrective action?
    • A. Corrective action fixes the bolt.
    • B. Correction is only paperwork.
    • C. They are identical.
    • D. Correction fixes the bolt; corrective action addresses why it was misplaced so that it does not recur.
  9. A site worker is new and has not received the required task training. What should be true before independent work?
    • A. Training is optional.
    • B. Competence is determined, training provided, and evidence retained.
    • C. Another worker is nearby.
    • D. The supervisor intends to train later.
  10. Which inputs might a project review or management review consider?
    • A. Only invoices.
    • B. Only photographs.
    • C. Customer feedback, nonconformities, provider performance, and project results.
    • D. Only the weather.
  11. A client changes the specification for a finish after procurement. What must occur?
    • A. Wait for final inspection.
    • B. Procurement ignores it.
    • C. The crew decides.
    • D. The requirement change is reviewed, documents updated, and affected people informed.
  12. Who has authority to stop work when a nonconformity is found?
    • A. Nobody until the next audit.
    • B. Only the client.
    • C. Only head office.
    • D. People given that authority by the organization, which should be defined and communicated.

Meridian Build · 12 questions · 15 minutes · pass mark 80 percent (configurable)

Circle one letter for each question.

  1. A project involves a client, a design consultant, a local authority inspector, and neighbors. Why identify these parties in planning?
    • A. To determine their relevant requirements and keep them under review.
    • B. It is not needed.
    • C. For marketing only.
    • D. To send them invoices.
  2. Before work starts on a new site, which activity reflects operational planning?
    • A. Starting immediately.
    • B. Waiting for the client to plan.
    • C. Copying the previous project's plan unchanged.
    • D. Defining the requirements, acceptance criteria, resources, inspection and test points, and records for this project.
  3. The client repeatedly complains about late handover documentation. What does customer focus expect from leadership?
    • A. Make sure the client's requirements are understood and met, and act on what affects satisfaction.
    • B. Ignore it.
    • C. Blame the subcontractor.
    • D. Stop sending documentation.
  4. A crew member is unsure why hold points matter. What should the crew member understand?
    • A. Only inspectors need to know about them.
    • B. How their work affects conformity, and the consequences of covering work before inspection.
    • C. Hold points are optional.
    • D. Hold points are paperwork only.
  5. Bad weather may delay a critical pour. What does risk-based planning look for?
    • A. Ignore the forecast.
    • B. Actions planned in advance and proportionate to the effect, such as alternative dates, followed by a review of how they worked.
    • C. Pour regardless of conditions.
    • D. Hope for good weather.
  6. A site generator powering a test rig fails repeatedly. Which requirement area applies?
    • A. Quality policy.
    • B. Design and development.
    • C. Infrastructure needed for conforming work, including its maintenance.
    • D. Awareness.
  7. A design change is issued to the office, but the site team receives it a week later. What is the gap?
    • A. Communication of changes: what, when, to whom, and how.
    • B. Calibration.
    • C. Customer property.
    • D. Organizational context.
  8. The firm designs a temporary works item for a client. Which activity confirms that the design meets its input requirements before use?
    • A. Painting it.
    • B. Design review and verification, with records.
    • C. Billing the client.
    • D. Cleaning the site.
  9. Precast units are delivered with no markings, and two similar types are on site. What is at risk?
    • A. Awareness.
    • B. Identification and traceability of outputs.
    • C. Pricing.
    • D. Quality policy.
  10. After handover, the client reports a defect within the warranty period. Which requirement area covers this?
    • A. Calibration.
    • B. Organizational context.
    • C. Post-delivery activities, such as warranty work.
    • D. Internal audit.
  11. The contractor never asks clients for feedback after handover. What is missing?
    • A. Calibration.
    • B. Monitoring of how customers perceive whether their needs and expectations were met.
    • C. Training.
    • D. Quality policy.
  12. An internal audit finds missing inspection records on two projects. What should management do?
    • A. Ignore the finding.
    • B. Close it without action.
    • C. Take correction and corrective action without undue delay and check that the actions worked.
    • D. Dismiss the auditor.
