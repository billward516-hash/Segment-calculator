Document Control Relay: facilitator sheet

Meridian Group. Teams of 5 to 6. 25 to 30 minutes. Module 4 (Clause 7.5).

Materials per team

  • One copy of work instruction revision A for every member.
  • Two copies of revision B and one revision notice, held by the facilitator.
  • Twelve data slips (cut apart), twelve blank record cards, one revision log, one outbox (an envelope or tray).

Timing

MinuteStep
0 to 3Brief the task. State the exercise date: 5 October 2026. Teams may organize themselves as they wish. Do not mention revisions.
3 to 10Teams complete cards to revision A.
10Hand revision B and the revision notice to two members of each team only, quietly. Say nothing to the rest of the team.
10 to 18Teams continue. Observe whether the two members tell the others, record the change, and remove revision A copies.
18Stop. Teams hand in the outbox, the revision log, and all copies of the instruction.
18 to 30Score and debrief.

Scoring

Work instruction WI-DC-01, revision A

DocumentWI-DC-01 Completing the work item record cardRevisionA
OwnerQuality coordinator, Meridian GroupEffectiveStart of exercise
Approved byJ. Doe (fictional)StatusCONTROLLED COPY

Purpose

To record the check result for each work item in the same way every time.

Steps

  1. Use a blue or black pen. Write in capital letters.
  2. Write the date as DD/MM/YYYY. Use the exercise date given by the facilitator.
  3. Copy the work item ID exactly from the data slip.
  4. Write the quantity in figures.
  5. Compare the measured value on the data slip with the limit. A value equal to or below the limit is within the limit. Circle PASS if it is within the limit. Circle FAIL if it is above the limit.
  6. If FAIL, write HOLD in the Remarks box.
  7. Initial the Inspector box. Write the time completed as hh:mm.
  8. Leave the Verified box blank.
  9. Fold the card in half along the dashed line, printed side out. Place it in the outbox.

DocumentWI-DC-01 Completing the work item record cardRevisionB
OwnerQuality coordinator, Meridian GroupEffectiveOn issue (time: ________ )
Approved byJ. Doe (fictional)StatusCONTROLLED COPY

Purpose

To record the check result for each work item in the same way every time.

Steps

  1. Use a blue or black pen. Write in capital letters.
  2. Write the date as YYYY-MM-DD. Use the exercise date given by the facilitator.
  3. Copy the work item ID exactly from the data slip.
  4. Write the quantity in figures, then in words in brackets. Example: 12 (TWELVE).
  5. Compare the measured value on the data slip with the limit. A value equal to or below the limit is within the limit. Circle PASS if it is within the limit. Circle FAIL if it is above the limit.
  6. If FAIL, write HOLD and the measured value in the Remarks box. Example: HOLD 5.6.
  7. Initial the Inspector box. Write the time completed as hh:mm.
  8. Ask a second team member to check the card against the data slip and initial the Verified box.
  9. Do not fold the card. Place it flat in the outbox.

Debrief

QuestionExpected answer
Where were the uncontrolled copies?Revision A copies still in the hands of members who never saw the notice, and any copy that was not withdrawn or marked obsolete at the end.
How would this occur on the floor?Printed copies kept at a workstation, personal notes, downloaded files, or a change briefed to one shift only. Clause 7.5.3 expects the current version at the point of use and prevention of unintended use of obsolete documents.
What did the revision log give your team?A record of what changed, when, and who received it. It allows anyone to confirm that they hold the current revision.
What is the cost?Mixed output that must be checked and reworked, held work, possible escapes to the customer, and lost time. Compare correct card counts between teams.
Which requirement does this exercise relate to?Clause 7.5 (documented information), mainly 7.5.3 (control), with links to 7.4 (communication) and 8.5.6 (control of changes).
