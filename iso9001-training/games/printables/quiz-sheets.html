Knowledge check: operator

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Pass mark: 80%

  1. Which supports preservation of outputs during processing?
    A. Appropriate handling, packaging, storage, and protection.
    B. Storing items wherever space is free.
    C. Leaving items exposed.
    D. Using unlabelled containers.
  2. A repeated minor defect is reworked each time but never reported. What is the weakness?
    A. The defect must be hidden.
    B. Rework is not allowed.
    C. The cause is never identified, so the nonconformity is likely to recur.
    D. Reports are only for major defects.
  3. Which control helps prevent unintended use of obsolete documented information?
    A. Letting each person decide.
    B. Removing it from points of use or identifying it clearly if retained for any purpose.
    C. Leaving it in the work area for reference.
    D. Storing it in a personal drawer.
  4. An auditor asks a question you cannot answer. What is the best response?
    A. Ask the auditor to come back after the audit.
    B. Change the subject to a process you know well.
    C. Give the most likely answer to avoid delay.
    D. State that you do not know and will find out, then refer to the supervisor; do not guess.
  5. During an audit, an operator is asked how their work affects quality objectives. What does this test?
    A. The operator's financial knowledge.
    B. The operator's seniority.
    C. Awareness of how personal work contributes to the QMS and its objectives.
    D. The calibration status of tools.
  6. Material, data, or equipment supplied by a customer for use in the work is best described as what?
    A. A provider output that needs no special care.
    B. Free stock that may be used for any purpose.
    C. Internal inventory once it has been received.
    D. Customer property that must be identified, protected, and reported if lost or damaged.
  7. Which statement describes awareness under the QMS?
    A. People are aware of the policy, relevant objectives, their contribution, and the implications of not conforming.
    B. People are aware only of customer names.
    C. People can recite the standard.
    D. People know only their job title.
  8. A shift ends and the incoming team is not told that a work item is on hold. Which area has failed?
    A. Management review inputs.
    B. Calibration traceability.
    C. Internal communication about matters relevant to the QMS.
    D. Corrective action effectiveness.
  9. An operator finds an old printed instruction stapled to a machine that differs from the current version. What is the right action?
    A. Photograph it and ignore it.
    B. Leave it, since people know which to follow.
    C. Remove or flag it and report it so that only the current version is available at the point of use.
    D. Update it by hand with pen.
  10. An order arrives with a changed specification. What must occur before processing?
    A. Wait for the customer to confirm receipt of the original.
    B. Pass the change only to the sales team.
    C. Review the requirement change and confirm the company can meet it; inform affected people.
    D. Process it as usual and update the paperwork later.
  11. Two work items of different status look identical and are placed in the same container. What control is lacking?
    A. Design verification.
    B. Management review.
    C. Customer property control.
    D. Identification of status with respect to monitoring and measurement requirements.
  12. Which clauses of ISO 9001 contain the auditable requirements?
    A. Clauses 0 through 5.
    B. Clauses 1 through 3.
    C. Clauses 6 through 10 only.
    D. Clauses 4 through 10.
  13. A nonconformity occurs. What must the organization do first?
    A. React to it and, as applicable, take action to control and correct it and deal with consequences.
    B. Wait for management review.
    C. Wait for an audit.
    D. Hide the evidence.
  14. An operator suggests a better way to stage tools that would reduce handling errors. How is this best treated?
    A. As something to implement immediately without review.
    B. As an improvement opportunity to be reviewed and, if suitable, introduced through planned change.
    C. As a matter only for the quality manager.
    D. As criticism of the supervisor that should be ignored.
  15. A work item is released while a final check discrepancy is open, without approval. Which requirement is breached?
    A. Design input requirements.
    B. Customer communication requirements.
    C. Release controls; release requires satisfactory completion of arrangements or authorized approval.
    D. Management review scheduling.
  16. A measuring instrument or test tool shows that its calibration or verification date has passed. What should be done?
    A. Use it only for non-critical checks.
    B. Adjust the date label to the next scheduled week.
    C. Use it carefully and note the overdue date on the record.
    D. Do not use it; flag it out and notify the supervisor or the calibration owner.
  17. A work item fails a check and is about to be released to the customer. First action?
    A. Move it to the end of the queue for later review.
    B. Quietly rework it and release it without a record.
    C. Release it and inform the customer afterward.
    D. Stop the release, flag and hold the work item, and notify the supervisor and quality.
  18. Who is accountable for the effectiveness of the QMS?
    A. The internal audit team.
    B. Top management.
    C. The quality manager alone.
    D. The certification body.
  19. Why are training records retained?
    A. They list the cost of each course.
    B. They replace the need for performance observation.
    C. They are evidence of competence.
    D. They are required only for new employees.
  20. An operator records a measurement of an item but is unsure whether it passed. What is the right step?
    A. Mark it as passed.
    B. Check against defined acceptance criteria or ask for guidance before recording or releasing.
    C. Record it as failed without check.
    D. Leave the field blank.

Knowledge check: technician

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Pass mark: 80%

  1. Which statement about the quality policy is correct?

    D. It is confidential and held only by management.
  2. Which is required before releasing products and services to the customer?
    A. Releasing first and inspecting later.
    B. Relying on customer inspection.
    C. Implementing planned arrangements at appropriate stages to verify requirements are met.
    D. Skipping checks when schedules are tight.
  3. A shared software tool crashes repeatedly and delays checks. What should be recognized?
    A. Only customers should be informed.
    B. Infrastructure must be maintained so that conforming output is not put at risk.
    C. Delays are acceptable if outputs are correct.
    D. Software problems are outside the QMS.
  4. A measuring instrument is found out of tolerance at calibration. What must the organization evaluate?
    A. The validity of earlier measurements made with it and any affected outputs.
    B. Only measurements taken after the calibration.
    C. Whether the calibration laboratory used the correct label.
    D. Only the cost of recalibrating the instrument.
  5. Which statement describes awareness under the QMS?
    A. People know only their job title.
    B. People are aware only of customer names.
    C. People can recite the standard.
    D. People are aware of the policy, relevant objectives, their contribution, and the implications of not conforming.
  6. An order arrives with a changed specification. What must occur before processing?
    A. Process it as usual and update the paperwork later.
    B. Pass the change only to the sales team.
    C. Wait for the customer to confirm receipt of the original.
    D. Review the requirement change and confirm the company can meet it; inform affected people.
  7. Which step in corrective action evaluates the need to eliminate causes?
    A. Closing the record immediately.
    B. Reviewing and analyzing the nonconformity and determining its causes and whether similar ones exist.
    C. Writing a report only.
    D. Retraining without analysis.
  8. A work item owned by the customer is damaged while stored. What should be done?
    A. Scrap it.
    B. Repair it quietly and return it.
    C. Report it to the customer and retain a record of what occurred.
    D. Replace it without telling the customer.
  9. A work item at the final check shows a characteristic marginally outside acceptance criteria. The customer is waiting. What is appropriate?
    A. Adjust the criteria locally.
    B. Hold it and seek an authorized disposition, which may include a concession from the customer.
    C. Re-measure until it passes.
    D. Release it to meet the deadline.
  10. What is the purpose of a process approach in the QMS?
    A. To assign every task to a separate department.
    B. To remove the need for records.
    C. To replace measurement with observation.
    D. To manage interacting activities as processes with defined inputs, outputs, and responsibilities.
  11. During a shift, a technician sees a process drifting toward limits but no one is assigned to decide on stopping it. What gap does this show?
    A. Responsibility and authority for process decisions were not clearly assigned or communicated.
    B. The audit program is too large.
    C. The customer survey is out of date.
    D. The calibration schedule is too frequent.
  12. Which best defines a corrective action?
    A. An action that repairs the single affected work item.
    B. An action that records the problem in a log.
    C. An action to eliminate the cause so the nonconformity does not recur.
    D. An action that informs the customer of the problem.
  13. Material, data, or equipment supplied by a customer for use in the work is best described as what?
    A. Free stock that may be used for any purpose.
    B. Customer property that must be identified, protected, and reported if lost or damaged.
    C. Internal inventory once it has been received.
    D. A provider output that needs no special care.
  14. Which supports preservation of outputs during processing?
    A. Leaving items exposed.
    B. Using unlabelled containers.
    C. Appropriate handling, packaging, storage, and protection.
    D. Storing items wherever space is free.
  15. What is the purpose of continual improvement?
    A. To reduce all costs.
    B. To enhance the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the QMS.
    C. To change the policy every month.
    D. To remove audits.
  16. Who is accountable for the effectiveness of the QMS?
    A. The quality manager alone.
    B. The certification body.
    C. Top management.
    D. The internal audit team.
  17. Root cause analysis concludes "operator error." What is the best next step?
    A. Close the investigation and retrain the individual.
    B. Ask why the system allowed the error (instruction, training, tooling, workload, design).
    C. Reassign the operator to other duties.
    D. Record it as a one-off and take no action.
  18. Which is typically a defined element when describing a process?
    A. The private opinions of the process owner.
    B. The seniority of the staff member.
    C. The color of the forms used.
    D. Inputs, outputs, resources, responsibilities, and performance indicators.
  19. A controlled work environment condition (for example temperature, cleanliness, system availability, or staffing) moves outside its limits. Which clause area addresses the environment?
    A. Customer property.
    B. Environment for the operation of processes.
    C. Design and development outputs.
    D. Management review.
  20. Who is responsible for knowing the quality policy?
    A. Only top management.
    B. Only the quality department.
    C. Everyone, to the extent it relates to their work.
    D. Only people who interact with customers.
