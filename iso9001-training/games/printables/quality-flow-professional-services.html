Quality Flow: printed version

Meridian Advisory (Professional, financial, and education services). Work item term: engagement. Seed 12345, 12 rounds.

Round sequence

  1. Invest (2 minutes). Teams spend Quality Points (QP) on control cards. Start with 20 QP and gain 1 QP each round.
  2. Event. The facilitator turns over the next event card from the deck order on the facilitator key.
  3. Process. Release 4 engagements to the first stage. At each stage move up to its capacity. Roll two dice for each engagement using the lookup table. Apply the event.
  4. Disposition. Hold flags go on detected defects. Choose redo or scrap and record it.
  5. Score. Update the tracking sheet. Every third round, teams with control C10 pick a past event and halve its impact.

Economics

Good engagement delivered+10
Scrapped-6 (-2 more if the defect arose at or after the constraint)
Redo (cost, 70% success)-2
Escape (complaint)-15 and reputation -1
Reputation at 0Supply on hold: remaining output earns half value

Final score = revenue + audit points (maximum 24) + reputation × 3 − penalties.
