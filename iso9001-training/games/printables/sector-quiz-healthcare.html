Circle one letter for each question.

  1. A ward uses a protocol sheet that is not the current approved version. What is the management system issue?
    • A. An obsolete document is in use at the point of use.
    • B. Training only.
    • C. Staffing only.
    • D. There is no issue.
  2. A measuring device used to record observations is past its verification date. What is the appropriate management response?
    • A. Remove the date label.
    • B. Ask staff to estimate.
    • C. Continue to use it.
    • D. Remove it from use, flag it, and assess the validity of earlier readings.
  3. Patient records are visible to staff who do not need access. Which requirement area is relevant?
    • A. Continual improvement only.
    • B. Context only.
    • C. Internal audit only.
    • D. Protection of customer property, which includes personal information entrusted to the organization.
  4. A handover record is missing sections for several cases. What is the quality management system concern?
    • A. Controlled delivery and release, with records showing requirements were met before handover.
    • B. Parking.
    • C. Appearance.
    • D. Pay.
  5. An externally supplied item arrives without the required documentation. What is appropriate?
    • A. Hold it and verify it against requirements before use.
    • B. Return it later.
    • C. Keep it with a note.
    • D. Use it.
  6. An agency staff member is scheduled for a task that requires a specific qualification. What does the organization need?
    • A. A badge only.
    • B. Confirmation of competence and retained evidence.
    • C. Nothing.
    • D. A verbal assurance.
  7. A change to the patient transfer documents is proposed. What should occur?
    • A. Wait for an incident.
    • B. Review consequences, authorize, communicate, and record.
    • C. Change it on the ward.
    • D. Announce it afterward.
  8. A near-miss is reported. Which response supports improvement?
    • A. Hide it.
    • B. Review it, determine whether a cause needs action, and track the action.
    • C. Discipline the reporter.
    • D. File it.
  9. Patient and family feedback is collected but never analyzed. Which requirement is not met?
    • A. Monitoring customer perception, and analyzing and evaluating the data.
    • B. Design review.
    • C. Risk planning.
    • D. Communication planning.
  10. Which statement about this program and clinical care is correct?
    • A. It replaces clinical guidance.
    • B. It overrides legal requirements.
    • C. It addresses the quality management system only; clinical standards and law govern care.
    • D. It provides treatment advice.
  11. Internal audit findings in the ward are never reviewed by senior leaders. Which element is weak?
    • A. Document control.
    • B. Calibration.
    • C. Training.
    • D. Reporting audit results to relevant management and using them in management review.
  12. What does "hold" mean for a case documentation set with an unresolved discrepancy?
    • A. Archive it.
    • B. A status that prevents it from proceeding until it is reviewed and dispositioned.
    • C. Delete it.
    • D. Forward it.

Circle one letter for each question.

  1. Meridian Care serves patients, families, referring organizations, and funders. Why identify these parties?
    • A. For marketing only.
    • B. It is not needed.
    • C. To set prices.
    • D. To determine their requirements that are relevant to the quality management system, and to review them.
  2. Two departments each assume the other checks that equipment service records are complete. What is the system issue?
    • A. A design issue.
    • B. Responsibilities and authorities are not clearly assigned and communicated.
    • C. A calibration error.
    • D. There is no issue.
  3. A new administrator does not know how to report a records discrepancy. Which requirement area applies?
    • A. Infrastructure.
    • B. Customer property.
    • C. Design and development.
    • D. Awareness, including how to contribute and the effect of not following the system.
  4. Which is a measurable objective for an outpatient clinic's administrative service?
    • A. "Improve."
    • B. "Do better than last year."
    • C. "Send at least 95 percent of appointment letters within two working days, reviewed monthly."
    • D. "Be caring."
  5. The scheduling system has no fallback process if it fails. What should management do?
    • A. Assess the risk to service delivery, plan proportionate actions, and review their effect.
    • B. Buy any system available.
    • C. Ignore it.
    • D. Wait.
  6. The air conditioning in a records storage room fails repeatedly. Which requirement area applies?
    • A. Quality policy.
    • B. Organizational context.
    • C. Infrastructure, including its maintenance.
    • D. Design and development.
  7. A referral form exists in several uncontrolled local versions. What should happen?
    • A. Keep all versions.
    • B. Let each team pick one.
    • C. Withdraw obsolete versions and make the current approved version available where it is used.
    • D. Wait for a complaint.
  8. A family member raises a concern about a delayed appointment letter. What should the receptionist do?
    • A. Ignore it.
    • B. Promise a fix without recording it.
    • C. Record the concern and route it through the feedback and complaint process.
    • D. Tell them to write a letter.
  9. A laundry contractor repeatedly delivers late. What should the organization do?
    • A. Evaluate and monitor the provider's performance against defined criteria and act on the results.
    • B. Stop checking deliveries.
    • C. Nothing.
    • D. Pay more.
  10. The internal audit programme never covers the appointment booking process. What is the weakness?
    • A. Only external auditors cover it.
    • B. There is no weakness.
    • C. The audit programme should take account of the importance of processes, changes, and previous results.
    • D. The process is too small to matter.
  11. Management review minutes list topics but record no decisions or actions. What is missing?
    • A. More attendees.
    • B. A new template only.
    • C. A longer meeting.
    • D. Outputs such as decisions on improvement, changes needed, and resources.
  12. Staff suggest a simpler checklist for record handover. What does continual improvement expect?
    • A. Ignore suggestions.
    • B. Consider the suggestion, evaluate it through change control, and act where it improves results.
    • C. Wait for the audit.
    • D. Change it on the ward without review.
