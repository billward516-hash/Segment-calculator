  1. Form groups of four: operator, technician, supervisor, manager. Fictional roles only.
  2. The facilitator reads a scenario card aloud.
  3. Each player reads the option card for their role and chooses A, B or C in secret (write the letter and fold the paper).
  4. Reveal choices. The facilitator reads the consequence of each path from the key.

CU01: The deadline and the open discrepancy

Clause link (2026, paraphrased): 5.1.1, 8.6, 8.7 (High, unverified)

Scenario. A shipment is due to leave in two hours. Final check has found a discrepancy on one work item in the batch. The cause is not yet known. The customer has already called twice about the delivery.

Role option cards (cut along the dashed lines)

Operator
You found the discrepancy.
  1. Flag and hold the work item, and tell the supervisor at once.
  2. Tell a colleague and carry on with the next item.
  3. Leave it in the batch; someone at final release will notice.
Technician
You are asked to look at the discrepancy.
  1. Check the measuring method and record the actual results, whatever they show.
  2. Give a quick opinion without recording anything.
  3. Suggest the instrument is probably wrong and the item is fine.
Supervisor
The operator brings you the held item.
  1. Thank the operator, keep the hold, and escalate to decide on the customer and the delivery.
  2. Keep the hold but tell the operator they should have caught it earlier.
  3. Release the item to meet the date and deal with it later.
Manager
The supervisor calls you about the late shipment.
  1. Back the hold, call the customer yourself, and agree a partial or late delivery.
  2. Ask for the decision to be made quickly and leave it to the supervisor.
  3. Ask whether the item really needs to be held.
