    • A. It is cosmetic.
    • B. Customer property.
    • C. There is no issue.
    • D. An obsolete document is in use at the point of use.

    • A. Control of monitoring and measuring resources and of changes.
    • B. Scope.

    • A. Release only after planned verification is complete, or with authorized approval.

  4. Client documents are stored in a shared folder accessible by unrelated staff. Which requirement is relevant?
    • A. Calibration.
    • B. Change control.
    • C. Protection of customer property, including personal and confidential information.
    • D. Awareness.
  5. A client changes the scope mid-engagement. What should happen?
    • A. Review the change, confirm capability, update records, and inform the team.
    • B. Ignore it.
    • C. Wait.
    • D. Continue as before.
  6. A subcontracted specialist delivers work without any evaluation of competence. Which control applies?
    • A. Calibration.
    • B. Internal audit only.
    • C. None.
    • D. External provider evaluation, requirements, and verification.
  7. A trainer delivers a course with no qualification record. Which requirement is not met?
    • A. Improvement.
    • B. Organizational context.
    • C. Monitoring.
    • D. Competence on the basis of education, training, or experience, with evidence.
  8. The same drafting error appears in three reports. Each report is corrected. What is missing?
    • A. A new report.
    • B. More editing.
    • C. Cause investigation, corrective action, and an effectiveness check.
    • D. A warning email.
  9. Client feedback surveys are collected and discussed at management review with actions. What does this evidence?
    • A. Traceability.
    • B. Calibration.
    • C. Design control.
    • D. Monitoring of customer perception, with analysis feeding management review.
  10. A partner proposes issuing a report before peer review to meet a deadline. What is the correct response?
    • A. Issue half.
    • B. Hold it until the planned review is complete or release is authorized by the person with authority.
    • C. Issue it and review it later.
    • D. Issue it.
  11. Which statement best describes risk-based thinking?
    • A. A mandatory formal risk method.
    • B. A concern for managers only.
    • C. Considering risks and opportunities that affect conformity and customer satisfaction, with actions proportionate to their potential impact.
    • D. A safety matter only.
  12. Records are completed from memory days after the activity. What is the main risk?
    • A. None.
    • B. Lower cost.
    • C. Inaccurate records that cannot serve as reliable objective evidence.
    • D. Faster work.

  1. Meridian Advisory adds online training courses to its services. What must be reviewed?
    • A. Whether the quality management system scope covers the new service and its processes.
    • B. Only the fees.
    • C. Nothing.
    • D. Only the marketing material.
  2. A consultant is asked how the quality policy affects her work. Which answer shows awareness?
    • A. "It is for managers."
    • B. "It commits us to meeting client requirements, so I complete the review step before issuing reports."
    • C. "I have not seen it."
    • D. "It is a poster."
  3. Nobody is assigned to report on quality management system performance to top management. What is missing?
    • A. Customer property.
    • B. Calibration.
    • C. An assigned responsibility and authority for reporting on system performance and opportunities for improvement.
    • D. A new template.
  4. The firm plans to merge two client service teams. What should the planning of this change address?
    • A. Only the new team name.
    • B. The purpose and consequences of the change, the integrity of the system, resources, and responsibilities.
    • C. Only office seating.
    • D. Nothing.
  5. A key reviewer is often unavailable, so reports sometimes skip review. What is the expected response?
    • A. Identify the risk, plan proportionate actions such as a trained backup reviewer, and check their effect.
    • B. Accept it.
    • C. Skip reviews when needed.
    • D. Hire anyone available.
  6. Lessons from past engagements are lost when staff leave. Which concept helps?
    • A. Customer property.
    • B. Organizational knowledge, maintained and made available.
    • C. Calibration.
    • D. Infrastructure.
  7. A deadline change agreed with the client is not shared with the drafting team. What is the gap?
    • A. Communication of relevant information to the people who need it.
    • B. Scope.
    • C. Quality policy.
    • D. Calibration.
  8. Before a proposal is sent to a prospective client, what should be confirmed?
    • A. The font.
    • B. Nothing.
    • C. That the service requirements are defined and the firm can meet the claims it makes.
    • D. Only the fee.
  9. A new training course is developed. Which activities confirm that it meets its intended learning outcomes?
    • A. Printing.
    • B. Review, verification, and validation, such as a pilot delivery, with records.
    • C. Advertising.
    • D. Pricing.
  10. An analyst completes a deliverable without the checklist the process specifies. What is missing?
    • A. Customer focus.
    • B. Controlled conditions, including available documented information and the defined checks.
    • C. Organizational context.
    • D. Design and development.
  11. An error is found in a report already sent to the client. What should happen first?
    • A. Do nothing unless the client notices.
    • B. Correct it silently.
    • C. Delete the record.
    • D. Report it so it can be controlled, the client informed as appropriate, and the cause considered.
  12. Which person is best placed to audit the reporting process?
    • A. The reporting team manager.
    • B. The author of the reports.
    • C. The client.
    • D. A trained auditor from another team, for objectivity.
