Circle one letter for each question.

  1. A team follows a deployment procedure from a shared-drive copy dated last year, while a newer version exists in the document repository. What is the issue?
    • A. An obsolete document is in use at the point of use.
    • B. It is a style issue only.
    • C. There is no issue.
    • D. Customer property.
  2. A test suite is changed and no one verifies that it still detects known defects. Which requirement area applies to tools used to verify conformity?
    • A. Context of the organization.
    • B. Quality policy.
    • C. Monitoring and measuring resources.
    • D. Management review.
  3. A defect is found on release day. The team proposes deploying and fixing it next week. What is required?
    • A. Delete the ticket.
    • B. Deploy to half the users.
    • C. Deploy.
    • D. Hold the release until the defect is dispositioned and release is authorized.
  4. Customer data is copied to a personal device for convenience. What is the issue?
    • A. Customer property must be protected and safeguarded.
    • B. It is a speed matter only.
    • C. It is only the customer's concern.
    • D. There is no issue.
  5. A production change is made without a change record. Which requirement is not met?
    • A. Policy wording.
    • B. Customer satisfaction.
    • C. Control of changes to service provision, with review and records.
    • D. Supplier price.
  6. A third-party library update introduces a defect. Which control applies?
    • A. Customer survey only.
    • B. Control of externally provided products and services, including evaluation and verification of updates.
    • C. Document control only.
    • D. Internal audit only.
  7. A release has a test sign-off, a reviewer, and a record of approval before deployment. This is evidence of:
    • A. Release control.
    • B. Context analysis.
    • C. Policy communication.
    • D. Awareness.
  8. A customer reports the same defect type for the third release. Retesting each release has not stopped it. What should happen?
    • A. Blame the developer.
    • B. Investigate the cause, act on it, and check effectiveness.
    • C. Close the ticket.
    • D. Retest more.
  9. An experienced engineer leaves and undocumented knowledge causes delays. Which concept helps?
    • A. Calibration.
    • B. Customer property.
    • C. Hold status.
    • D. Organizational knowledge: determining and maintaining the knowledge needed for processes.
  10. New staff start on-call duty without induction on incident procedures. Which requirement is not met?
    • A. Organizational context.
    • B. Competence.
    • C. Design review.
    • D. Internal audit.
  11. A manager asks a tester to adjust a recorded test result to avoid a delay. How should the tester respond?
    • A. Delete the record.
    • B. Adjust it.
    • C. Adjust it once.
    • D. Decline, record the actual result, and escalate.
  12. Which is an example of a measurable service quality objective?
    • A. "Be excellent."
    • B. "Have happy staff."
    • C. "Resolve 90 percent of priority incidents within the agreed target time."
    • D. "Do our best."

Circle one letter for each question.

  1. A cloud hosting provider changes its terms in a way that affects how Meridian Digital can deliver its service. Where should this be considered?
    • A. In the review of external issues that affect the organization's ability to achieve its intended results.
    • B. Only at the next release.
    • C. Nowhere.
    • D. Only in finance.
  2. The service desk is outsourced. Should it be considered in the quality management system?
    • A. No; outsourced processes fall outside the system.
    • B. Yes; the scope and the controls over externally provided processes must address it.
    • C. Only if the customer asks.
    • D. Only if it is cheaper.
  3. Nobody is named as owner of the release process. What is the risk?
    • A. Releases become faster.
    • B. Costs fall.
    • C. There is no risk.
    • D. Responsibilities and authorities for the process are unclear, so its results may not be achieved.
  4. A developer does not know that a skipped code review could reach customers as a defect. Which requirement area applies?
    • A. Design and development.
    • B. Customer property.
    • C. Infrastructure.
    • D. Awareness of the consequences of not following the system.
  5. One engineer holds the only access to a key production system. What is the expected response?
    • A. Do nothing.
    • B. Wait for an outage.
    • C. Identify this as a risk, plan proportionate actions, and then check their effect.
    • D. Share the access details by email.
  6. Monitoring alerts are so frequent that staff ignore them. What should be reviewed?
    • A. Nothing.
    • B. What needs to be monitored and measured, the methods used, and when results are evaluated.
    • C. Turning off all alerts.
    • D. Adding more alerts.
  7. A customer emails a feature request to a support agent. What should the agent do?
    • A. Delete it.
    • B. Ignore it.
    • C. Build it personally.
    • D. Record it and route it through the agreed customer communication process.
  8. A new feature is built without documented requirements or acceptance tests. Which control area is weak?
    • A. Customer property.
    • B. Organizational context.
    • C. Design and development inputs, reviews, verification, and validation.
    • D. Calibration.
  9. Sales promises a recovery time the platform has never achieved. What should have happened first?
    • A. Nothing.
    • B. Engineering finds out later.
    • C. A review of the requirement and confirmation of the ability to meet it before commitment.
    • D. The customer decides.
  10. After go-live, the contract requires a period of stabilization support. Which requirement area covers it?
    • A. Post-delivery activities.
    • B. Internal audit.
    • C. Quality policy.
    • D. Organizational context.
  11. Incident tickets are closed but never analyzed for trends. What is missing?
    • A. More tickets.
    • B. Analysis and evaluation of the data to find recurring issues and improvement opportunities.
    • C. A new tool.
    • D. Nothing.
  12. A tester finds a nonconformity in a released build. What is the tester's first responsibility?
    • A. Report it promptly so it can be controlled and its cause considered.
    • B. Wait for the customer to notice.
    • C. Keep it quiet.
    • D. Fix production directly without a record.
