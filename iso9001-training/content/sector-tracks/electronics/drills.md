# Sector Drills: Electronics and Semiconductor

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

These drills extend the electronics and semiconductor sector quiz with Translate items and a scenario sort. They are untimed. In the Sector Casebook (Drills), each answer shows feedback at once and missed items return until they are secure; the pass mark for the track is 80 percent on the first try. Option order is shuffled on screen. Clause tags use 2015 numbering and are unverified. **[VERIFY]** Role tags: F frontline, T technical, S supervisor.

## Part 1: Multiple-choice items

**EL-D01.** Particle alarms are logged with no response entries. Which requirement is affected?

- A) Policy
- B) Context
- C) Environment for the operation of processes
- D) Design

**Answer: C.** Excursions must be responded to. *(Cl. 7.1.4; F, T)*

**EL-D02.** A thickness gauge failed calibration after measuring a lot. What must happen?

- A) Assess earlier results and act on affected lots
- B) Adjust and continue
- C) Replace the label
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Look-back review is required. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**EL-D03.** A child lot is merged and ships under another lot number. What breaks?

- A) Competence
- B) Leadership
- C) Objectives
- D) Traceability

**Answer: D.** Identity must be kept through merges. *(Cl. 8.5.2; T)*

**EL-D04.** Two customers' wafers are staged together without labels. What is affected?

- A) Calibration
- B) Customer property and identification
- C) Design
- D) Planning

**Answer: B.** Keep customer material identified and separate. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**EL-D05.** Six operators can edit production recipes without approval. What is the risk?

- A) Faster work only
- B) Uncontrolled changes to the process
- C) Nothing
- D) Higher yield

**Answer: B.** Recipe changes need control. *(Cl. 8.5.6; T, S)*

**EL-D06.** A cheaper robot pad was fitted without a trial and scratches began. What was missing?

- A) More operator care
- B) More inspection only
- C) Nothing
- D) Review of the change before use

**Answer: D.** Changes affecting production are reviewed. *(Cl. 8.5.6; T)*

**EL-D07.** A gauge is calibrated but uses 30 percent of the tolerance. What should you ask?

- A) Whether it can detect the effect of a process change
- B) Nothing
- C) Whether it is clean
- D) Whether it is new

**Answer: A.** Capability differs from calibration. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**EL-D08.** Outgoing inspection checked 2 of 25 wafers after an excursion. What is the gap?

- A) None
- B) Too many checks
- C) Sampling did not respond to a known risk
- D) Packaging

**Answer: C.** Release checks should be able to detect the problem. *(Cl. 8.6; T)*

**EL-D09.** An instruction at the polisher is revision F; G is current. What is right?

- A) Use both
- B) Continue
- C) Replace it with revision G and withdraw F
- D) Mark it later

**Answer: C.** Current documents at the point of use. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**EL-D10.** A manager says month-end matters more than three out-of-limit wafers. Which clause?

- A) Leadership and commitment
- B) Calibration
- C) Context
- D) Design

**Answer: A.** Targets must not override controls. *(Cl. 5.1.1; S)*

**EL-D11.** Who may approve shipping wafers outside limits?

- A) The production manager alone
- B) The shipping clerk
- C) Nobody ever
- D) The defined authority and, where the terms say so, the customer in writing

**Answer: D.** Authority and customer terms. *(Cl. 8.7, 8.2.3; S)*

**EL-D12.** A process change needs 90 days' customer notice. What must the change record include?

- A) Nothing
- B) A customer notification step
- C) A discount
- D) A new logo

**Answer: B.** Customer change terms must be met. *(Cl. 8.2.3; S)*

**EL-D13.** Filter maintenance is postponed for parts. What should happen?

- A) Ignore it
- B) Escalate and assess the effect on the environment
- C) Run faster
- D) Remove the filter

**Answer: B.** Infrastructure maintenance supports conformity. *(Cl. 7.1.3; T, S)*

**EL-D14.** A technician is unsure whether to record an alarm response. What does this suggest?

- A) The technician is careless
- B) Nothing
- C) A design issue
- D) Awareness of the procedure needs strengthening

**Answer: D.** Awareness is part of support. *(Cl. 7.3; S)*

**EL-D15.** Rework lots are named in two styles. What is the risk?

- A) Identification becomes unreliable
- B) None
- C) Faster rework
- D) Better yield

**Answer: A.** Consistent identification supports traceability. *(Cl. 8.5.2; T)*

**EL-D16.** A qualification matrix is current and interviews confirm knowledge. What is this?

- A) A nonconformity
- B) Not relevant
- C) Conforming evidence of competence
- D) An observation always

**Answer: C.** Competence recorded and shown. *(Cl. 7.2; F)*

**EL-D17.** Carriers use colors by product family, as the handling instruction allows. Is this a finding?

- A) Yes
- B) Yes, major
- C) No, it is a defined identification aid
- D) Only in audits

**Answer: C.** Defined color coding conforms. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F)*

**EL-D18.** A scratch defect is closed as "handling" without looking at position data. What is missing?

- A) Testing the cause against the data
- B) A longer report
- C) A new form
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Analysis precedes the cause decision. *(Cl. 10.2; T)*

## Part 2: Translate items

A Translate item asks you to restate a general requirement in the language of this sector, or to restate a sector example as the underlying requirement.

**EL-T01.** Which example restates "the environment for operations is maintained"?

- A) The office has plants
- B) Parking is available
- C) Gowns are blue
- D) Particle excursions trigger a recorded response and filter changes are on schedule

**Answer: D.** Cleanroom control is the sector form. *(Cl. 7.1.4; F)*

**EL-T02.** "Lot L-883B carries a hand-written number with no split record." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy
- B) Identification and traceability
- C) Management review
- D) Objectives

**Answer: B.** Unrecorded splits break identity. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F)*

**EL-T03.** Which example restates "measuring resources are suitable for the measurement"?

- A) The gauge is new
- B) A flatness gauge has a current capability study for the product being measured
- C) The gauge is clean
- D) The gauge is expensive

**Answer: B.** Suitability means capability. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**EL-T04.** "Polish pressure is raised by 10 percent without updating the risk analysis." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Customer property
- B) Audit planning
- C) Context
- D) Changes are planned and risks considered

**Answer: D.** Change planning and risk. *(Cl. 6.1, 8.5.6; T)*

**EL-T05.** Which example restates "customer property is identified and safeguarded"?

- A) Each customer's wafers are staged separately with labels and a register
- B) All wafers share a shelf
- C) Wafers are counted weekly
- D) Labels are optional

**Answer: A.** Customer wafers are customer property. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**EL-T06.** "Outgoing sampling is the same after an excursion as on a normal day." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Competence
- B) Policy
- C) Release verification must be suitable for the risk
- D) Knowledge

**Answer: C.** Verification responds to risk. *(Cl. 8.6; T)*
