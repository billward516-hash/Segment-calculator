## 1. The sector at a glance

Logistics organizations receive, store, pick, load, and deliver goods that usually belong to their customers. The work item is the order or shipment. Quality depends on pallet and order identity, correct locations, scanning that verifies the item, verified scales and temperature loggers, protection of customer goods, controlled carriers, and dispatch release against checks.

**Process map (Meridian Logistics).** Receiving → Put-away and storage → Picking and packing → Loading check → Final dispatch check → Delivery and proof of delivery. The work item is the **order**. The constraint stage, where work most often queues, is picking and packing.

| Stage | Typical inputs | Typical outputs | Typical owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Receiving | Inbound goods, advance shipping notices, customer specifications | Received pallets with labels and discrepancy records | Receiving lead |
| 2. Put-away and storage | Received pallets, slotting plan, storage rules | Stored goods in recorded locations | Inventory controller |
| 3. Picking and packing | Orders, pick instructions, scanners | Picked and packed orders | Pick team leader |
| 4. Loading check | Packed orders, load plan, scales | Loaded trailers with load checks | Loading supervisor |
| 5. Final dispatch check | Load checks, order status, discrepancies | Released dispatch or held load | Dispatch manager |
| 6. Delivery and proof of delivery | Released loads, carriers, delivery schedule | Delivered orders with proof of delivery | Transport planner |

Every pallet carries an identity that must match the system. In a records trail, compare labels, location history, pick confirmations, and load checks.

## 4. Typical nonconformities and their usual causes

Each likely cause is a system condition, not a person. When a cause names a person ("careless", "forgot"), ask what in the system allowed the event and what would prevent it for anyone in that role.

| No. | Nonconformity | Clause | Likely root cause (system condition) | Preventive control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wrong item picked from a look-alike location | 8.5.1 | Pick confirmation scans location only | Product barcode scan |
| 2 | Pallet label does not match the system order | 8.5.2 | Labels batch-printed on a shared tray | Print at the lane per pallet |
| 3 | Temperature logger past verification | 7.1.5 | No due-date alert | Alerts |
| 4 | Damaged customer goods put away without notice | 8.5.3 | No damage step at receiving | Damage check and customer notice |

## 5. Sector specifics

### Customer property

Most goods in a warehouse belong to customers. Customer property also includes customer data and returnable packaging. Record damage on receipt, protect goods in storage, and inform the customer of loss or damage.

### External providers

Carriers, subcontracted drivers, equipment hire, and warehouse systems. Approve carriers, check vehicle suitability for the load, and monitor performance.

### Measuring and monitoring resources

Scales, scanners, temperature loggers, and tracking systems. A scanner that fails often leads to keyed entries; a scale past verification makes weight checks unreliable.

### Work environment

Temperature control, lighting, and congestion affect conformity. Warehouse safety is managed separately.

## 6. Sector vocabulary

| Term | Meaning in this primer |
|---|---|
| Order | A customer request for goods; the work item |
| Shipment | Goods sent together |
| Pallet | A platform holding goods, with a label |
| SSCC label | A standard pallet identity label |
| Location | A recorded storage place |
| Slotting | Assigning products to locations |
| Pick face | The location pickers take goods from |
| Pick confirmation | The scan or entry that confirms a pick |
| Keyed entry | Typing a code instead of scanning |
| Stock adjustment | A change to recorded stock quantity |
| Look-alike product | A product easily confused with another |
| Load check | Verification before dispatch |
| Weight variance | Difference between expected and actual weight |
| Dispatch release | The decision that a load may leave |
| Booking slot | An agreed delivery time at the destination |
| Advance shipping notice | A message describing a shipment before it arrives |
| Proof of delivery | Signed evidence of delivery |
| Returnable packaging | Crates or pallets to be returned to the owner |
| Cold store | Temperature-controlled storage |
| Temperature logger | A device recording temperature |
| Carrier | A transport provider |
| Trailer seal | A numbered seal showing a trailer was not opened |
