# Sector Drills: Logistics, Warehousing, and Distribution

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

These drills extend the logistics, warehousing, and distribution sector quiz with Translate items and a scenario sort. They are untimed. In the Sector Casebook (Drills), each answer shows feedback at once and missed items return until they are secure; the pass mark for the track is 80 percent on the first try. Option order is shuffled on screen. Clause tags use 2015 numbering and are unverified. **[VERIFY]** Role tags: F frontline, T technical, S supervisor.

## Part 1: Multiple-choice items

**LG-D01.** A picker uses an older pick instruction. What should happen?

- A) Continue
- B) Use both
- C) Replace it with the current issue
- D) Ask a colleague

**Answer: C.** Current instructions at the pick face. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**LG-D02.** A temperature logger is past its verification date. What is right?

- A) Withdraw or verify it and review recent records
- B) Keep using it
- C) Remove the label
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Verified monitoring devices. *(Cl. 7.1.5; F, T)*

**LG-D03.** Damaged customer goods are put into stock without notice. What is required?

- A) Hide the damage
- B) Repack quietly
- C) Nothing
- D) Record the damage and inform the customer

**Answer: D.** Customer property damage is reported. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**LG-D04.** A load is dispatched with an open count discrepancy and no authorization. What is affected?

- A) Policy
- B) Release
- C) Context
- D) Design

**Answer: B.** Release with discrepancy needs authorization. *(Cl. 8.6; F, S)*

**LG-D05.** Two pallets carry labels for a different order than the system shows. What is affected?

- A) Calibration
- B) Identification
- C) Competence
- D) Objectives

**Answer: B.** Labels must match records. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F)*

**LG-D06.** A stock adjustment removes 18 cases with reason "system". What is the gap?

- A) None
- B) Faster counting
- C) Cost
- D) Unexplained, unauthorized change to records

**Answer: D.** Adjustments need reasons. *(Cl. 8.5.2; T)*

**LG-D07.** Pick confirmations are keyed by hand because a scanner fails often. What is best?

- A) Repair the scanner and treat keyed picks as a risk
- B) Ignore it
- C) Ban keying with no repair
- D) Train harder

**Answer: A.** Measuring tools must work. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**LG-D08.** Look-alike products are placed next to each other after slotting. What was missing?

- A) Lighting
- B) Picker care
- C) Review of the slotting change against the look-alike list
- D) Nothing

**Answer: C.** Changes are reviewed. *(Cl. 8.5.6; S)*

**LG-D09.** Customer terms require goods in monitored aisles; the plan moves them to an aisle with no logger. What is required?

- A) Move them anyway
- B) Tell the customer later
- C) Meet the customer terms before approving the plan
- D) Add a sign

**Answer: C.** Customer requirements. *(Cl. 8.2.3; S)*

**LG-D10.** Load weight variance is 9 percent against a 3 percent limit and the load is released. What is wrong?

- A) Release after a failed check without authorization
- B) Nothing
- C) The scale
- D) The trailer

**Answer: A.** Checks before release. *(Cl. 8.6; S)*

**LG-D11.** A dispatcher says "nobody counts three cases" to justify sending a short load. Which clause?

- A) Calibration
- B) Context
- C) Design
- D) Leadership and commitment

**Answer: D.** Pressure must not replace controls. *(Cl. 5.1.1; S)*

**LG-D12.** Returnable crates owned by a customer are stacked outside, unlabeled. What is required?

- A) Use them
- B) Identify, protect, and record them
- C) Scrap them
- D) Nothing

**Answer: B.** Customer property. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**LG-D13.** A carrier substitutes an unapproved vehicle for a temperature-controlled load. Which clause?

- A) Policy
- B) Control of external providers
- C) Context
- D) Design

**Answer: B.** Carriers are controlled. *(Cl. 8.4; S)*

**LG-D14.** Mislabels recur on peak days and are closed with reminders. What should the auditor look at?

- A) Who was on shift
- B) Radio use
- C) Nothing
- D) The labeling method and what changed

**Answer: D.** Patterns point to methods. *(Cl. 10.2; S)*

**LG-D15.** A risk note about batch printing has no owner after five weeks. Which clause?

- A) Actions to address risks
- B) Calibration
- C) Customer property
- D) Context

**Answer: A.** Raised risks are addressed. *(Cl. 6.1; S)*

**LG-D16.** Proof of delivery records are complete and retrievable. What is this?

- A) A nonconformity
- B) Not relevant
- C) Conforming evidence
- D) A risk

**Answer: C.** Delivery records in order. *(Cl. 8.5.5; F)*

**LG-D17.** Wrapping film is blue, which is allowed. Is this a finding?

- A) Yes
- B) Yes, minor
- C) No
- D) Only for some customers

**Answer: C.** Allowed practice. *(Cl. 8.5.4; F)*

**LG-D18.** Floor markings are faded and the safety team has a work order. Is it a quality finding?

- A) No, it is a safety matter already in hand
- B) Yes, major
- C) Yes, minor
- D) Only on audits

**Answer: A.** Outside this program. *(Cl. 7.1.4; F)*

## Part 2: Translate items

A Translate item asks you to restate a general requirement in the language of this sector, or to restate a sector example as the underlying requirement.

**LG-T01.** Which example restates "outputs are identified and traceable"?

- A) Pallets are wrapped
- B) Pallets are counted monthly
- C) Pallets are blue
- D) Each pallet label matches its order in the system from picking to delivery

**Answer: D.** Identity matches records. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F)*

**LG-T02.** "A load left 3 cases short with the release box ticked by the dispatcher." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Competence
- B) Release requires completed checks or authorized approval
- C) Policy
- D) Knowledge

**Answer: B.** Release control. *(Cl. 8.6; S)*

**LG-T03.** Which example restates "measuring resources are verified"?

- A) Loggers are new
- B) Cold store loggers carry current verification and records are checked
- C) Loggers are hidden
- D) Loggers are optional

**Answer: B.** Verification. *(Cl. 7.1.5; F)*

**LG-T04.** "Customer goods arrived damaged and were stored without telling the customer." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Calibration
- B) Objectives
- C) Context
- D) Customer property is reported when damaged

**Answer: D.** Customer property. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**LG-T05.** Which example restates "changes are reviewed before implementation"?

- A) A re-slotting plan is checked against customer storage terms and the look-alike list before approval
- B) Signs are updated
- C) The coffee machine moves
- D) Staff are told after

**Answer: A.** Change review. *(Cl. 8.5.6; S)*

**LG-T06.** "Pick confirmations accept a location scan without checking the product." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy
- B) Context
- C) Controlled conditions include suitable verification of the output
- D) Audit

**Answer: C.** Process control. *(Cl. 8.5.1; T)*
