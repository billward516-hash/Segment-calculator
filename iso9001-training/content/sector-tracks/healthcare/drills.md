# Sector Drills: Healthcare and Clinical Services (Management System Topics Only)

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

These drills extend the healthcare and clinical services (management system topics only) sector quiz with Translate items and a scenario sort. They are untimed. In the Sector Casebook (Drills), each answer shows feedback at once and missed items return until they are secure; the pass mark for the track is 80 percent on the first try. Option order is shuffled on screen. Clause tags use 2015 numbering and are unverified. **[VERIFY]** Role tags: F frontline, T technical, S supervisor.

## Part 1: Multiple-choice items

**HC-D01.** The ward folder holds an earlier version of a protocol sheet. What should happen?

- A) Use whichever is closer
- B) Keep both
- C) Replace it with the current version and withdraw the old one
- D) Wait for the audit

**Answer: C.** Current documents at the point of use. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**HC-D02.** A measuring device in use is past its verification date. What is the management system response?

- A) Remove it from use and refer the question of earlier readings to the device owner and responsible clinicians
- B) Keep using it carefully
- C) Remove the label
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Verified devices; clinical review is for clinicians. *(Cl. 7.1.5; F, T)*

**HC-D03.** Screens show patient records unattended. Which requirement is affected?

- A) Calibration
- B) Context
- C) Design
- D) Protection of customer property (personal information)

**Answer: D.** Personal information belongs to the patient. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**HC-D04.** Handover sheets have blank sections for pending results. What is affected?

- A) Policy only
- B) Controlled service delivery and records
- C) Nothing
- D) Purchasing

**Answer: B.** Handover records must be complete. *(Cl. 8.5.1; F)*

**HC-D05.** Agency staff on shift have current competence records. What is this?

- A) A nonconformity
- B) Conforming evidence
- C) Not relevant
- D) An observation always

**Answer: B.** Competence recorded. *(Cl. 7.2; S)*

**HC-D06.** A system change made a field mandatory and wards were not told. What is the gap?

- A) Staff care
- B) Nothing
- C) Calibration
- D) Change review and communication

**Answer: D.** Changes are reviewed and communicated. *(Cl. 8.5.6, 7.4; S)*

**HC-D07.** Rejected booking requests go to an inbox nobody monitors. What is needed?

- A) An owner and a process to act on failed requests
- B) More reminders
- C) A new inbox
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Failed outputs are identified and acted on. *(Cl. 8.7; S)*

**HC-D08.** Feedback shows the same comment three times with no action. What is this?

- A) Fine
- B) A clinical issue
- C) Feedback not analyzed and used
- D) A design input

**Answer: C.** Feedback is analyzed and used. *(Cl. 9.1.2; S)*

**HC-D09.** A colleague asks you to sign a discharge checklist with a listed review blank. What is the investigator's role?

- A) Make the clinical decision
- B) Sign it
- C) Ensure the organization's own process is followed and the named person decides
- D) Ignore it

**Answer: C.** The investigator protects the process, not the clinical decision. *(Cl. 8.5.1, 5.3; S)*

**HC-D10.** Discharge packs in stock are missing pages. Where should the investigator look next?

- A) The print order and the receipt check
- B) The patient
- C) The ward clock
- D) The car park

**Answer: A.** Follow outputs back to providers. *(Cl. 8.4; T)*

**HC-D11.** A recurring blank field is closed with "staff reminded". What should the auditor ask?

- A) Who forgot
- B) Nothing
- C) Why staff are busy
- D) Which route into the record allows the blank, and what changed

**Answer: D.** Analyze by route. *(Cl. 10.2; S)*

**HC-D12.** A revised form removes a section. What must the change include?

- A) Nothing
- B) A review of the risk of removing it
- C) A new colour
- D) A launch email only

**Answer: B.** Risks from changes are considered. *(Cl. 6.1; S)*

**HC-D13.** Old forms remain in stock after a revision. What is required?

- A) Use them up
- B) Withdraw or mark obsolete forms
- C) Nothing
- D) Keep them for later

**Answer: B.** Obsolete documents are controlled. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**HC-D14.** Nobody is named to return family calls. Which clause?

- A) Calibration
- B) Design
- C) Context
- D) Roles, responsibilities, and authorities

**Answer: D.** Assign responsibility. *(Cl. 5.3; S)*

**HC-D15.** A pilot shows blank fields and nobody reviews the results. What is missing?

- A) Review of change results before rollout
- B) Nothing
- C) More pilots
- D) A new form

**Answer: A.** Changes are reviewed. *(Cl. 8.5.6; S)*

**HC-D16.** A staff photo board is out of date. Is this a quality finding?

- A) Yes
- B) Yes, major
- C) No
- D) Only if public

**Answer: C.** Not a requirement. *(Cl. 7.4; F)*

**HC-D17.** Equipment service logs are up to date for all devices. What is this?

- A) A nonconformity
- B) Not relevant
- C) Conforming evidence of maintenance
- D) A risk

**Answer: C.** Maintenance recorded. *(Cl. 7.1.3; F)*

**HC-D18.** Feedback trends are an agenda item at management review. What does this show?

- A) Feedback is used at top level
- B) A nonconformity
- C) Nothing
- D) Too much reporting

**Answer: A.** Management review uses customer feedback. *(Cl. 9.3; S)*

## Part 2: Translate items

A Translate item asks you to restate a general requirement in the language of this sector, or to restate a sector example as the underlying requirement.

**HC-T01.** Which example restates "customer property is protected"?

- A) Records are stored forever
- B) Records are printed
- C) Records are shared widely
- D) Patient records are visible only to staff who need them and screens lock when unattended

**Answer: D.** Personal information protection. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**HC-T02.** "A blood pressure monitor is used past its verification date." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy
- B) Monitoring and measuring resources are verified at set intervals
- C) Context
- D) Audit

**Answer: B.** Device verification. *(Cl. 7.1.5; F)*

**HC-T03.** Which example restates "competence is determined and recorded"?

- A) Staff wear badges
- B) Agency staff competence is checked and recorded before the shift
- C) Staff sign in
- D) Staff take breaks

**Answer: B.** Competence records for all staff. *(Cl. 7.2; S)*

**HC-T04.** "Three different discharge times were given in one day with no reason recorded." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Calibration
- B) Design
- C) Context
- D) Controlled service delivery and communication with customers

**Answer: D.** Service delivery and communication. *(Cl. 8.5.1, 8.2.1; S)*

**HC-T05.** Which example restates "changes are reviewed and communicated"?

- A) A referral system change is tested and wards are briefed before it goes live
- B) The system is updated at night
- C) The change is emailed after
- D) Nothing is said

**Answer: A.** Change review and communication. *(Cl. 8.5.6; S)*

**HC-T06.** "Repeated letters about discharge times were answered one by one." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy
- B) Resources
- C) Recurring feedback leads to corrective action
- D) Context

**Answer: C.** Trends lead to corrective action. *(Cl. 10.2; S)*
