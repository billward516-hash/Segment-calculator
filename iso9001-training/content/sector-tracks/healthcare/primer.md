## 1. The sector at a glance

Healthcare organizations deliver services to patients in cases or episodes of care. From a management system view, quality depends on current approved documents and forms, verified measuring devices, complete handover and discharge records, protection of personal information, competent permanent and agency staff, reliable communication with patients and families, and feedback that is analyzed and used.

**Process map (Meridian Care).** Referral and intake → Assessment → Treatment or procedure → Documentation and results review → Discharge or handover check → Follow-up. The work item is the **case**. The constraint stage, where work most often queues, is treatment or procedure.

| Stage | Typical inputs | Typical outputs | Typical owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Referral and intake | Referrals, appointment requests, personal details | Registered case with intake record | Intake and booking team |
| 2. Assessment | Intake record, assessment forms, devices | Completed assessment record | Assessing professional |
| 3. Treatment or procedure | Plan of care, supplies, equipment, protocols | Delivered treatment or procedure with records | Treating team (clinical content outside this program) |
| 4. Documentation and results review | Records, results, documentation standards | Reviewed documentation and results | Responsible professional |
| 5. Discharge or handover check | Discharge checklist, handover forms, follow-up requests | Discharge or handover record; follow-up booked | Ward or service lead |
| 6. Follow-up | Follow-up requests, feedback, outcomes data | Follow-up appointment, feedback record | Service manager |

The records an auditor follows here are management system records: forms, checklists, handover sheets, booking logs, verification labels, and feedback records. The auditor does not judge clinical content.

## 4. Typical nonconformities and their usual causes

Each likely cause is a system condition, not a person. When a cause names a person ("careless", "forgot"), ask what in the system allowed the event and what would prevent it for anyone in that role.

| No. | Nonconformity | Clause | Likely root cause (system condition) | Preventive control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outdated protocol sheet in a ward folder | 7.5.3 | Revised documents emailed but not withdrawn | Withdraw old copies and brief at handover |
| 2 | Measuring device past its verification date in use | 7.1.5 | No due-date alert | Device register with alerts |
| 3 | Handover sheet missing pending tasks | 8.5.1 | Form allows sections to be left blank | Required fields or a prompt |
| 4 | Unattended screens showing personal records | 8.5.3 | No automatic screen lock | Automatic lock |
| 5 | Follow-up request lost after a system change | 8.5.6 | System change not communicated or tested | Change assessment and briefing |
| 6 | Rejected requests sent to an unmonitored inbox | 8.7 | No owner for failed requests | Monitored queue with owner |
| 7 | Discharge approval initialed with a listed review blank | 8.5.1 | Checklist allows approval with a blank line | Required review step |
| 8 | Feedback collected but not acted on | 9.1.2 | No owner for feedback analysis | Owner and review cycle |
| 9 | Incomplete printed information packs | 8.4 | No receipt check of printed material | Receipt check |
| 10 | Recurring blank field closed with reminders | 10.2 | Form design not examined | Analyze by route and fix the form |
| 11 | Family calls not returned | 5.3 | No one assigned to return calls | Assign responsibility |

## 5. Sector specifics

### Customer property

Patient property includes personal information, records, personal belongings, and samples. Protect it, give access only to those who need it, and report loss.

### External providers

Pharmaceutical and supply vendors, external laboratories, equipment service providers, print suppliers, and agency staff. Verify what they provide and check agency staff competence.

### Measuring and monitoring resources

Measuring devices, laboratory instruments, audit tools, and patient feedback surveys. Verify devices at set intervals; the device owner and responsible clinicians decide any clinical review of earlier readings.

### Work environment

Privacy, noise, staffing and workload, and infection control. Infection control is a clinical topic outside this program.

## 7. Sector schemes and local rules

Healthcare is governed by clinical standards, professional regulation, privacy law, and medical device rules, and may use accreditation schemes. None of these are taught here. This program covers the ISO 9001 quality management system only. Where a scheme or legal requirement applies, the organization remains responsible for it; consult the certification body and the relevant authority. **[VERIFY]**
