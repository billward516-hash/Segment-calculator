# Sector Drills: Professional, Financial, and Education Services

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

These drills extend the professional, financial, and education services sector quiz with Translate items and a scenario sort. They are untimed. In the Sector Casebook (Drills), each answer shows feedback at once and missed items return until they are secure; the pass mark for the track is 80 percent on the first try. Option order is shuffled on screen. Clause tags use 2015 numbering and are unverified. **[VERIFY]** Role tags: F frontline, T technical, S supervisor.

## Part 1: Multiple-choice items

**PS-D01.** A report is sent with no peer review record. Which requirement?

- A) Policy
- B) Context
- C) Release of products and services
- D) Calibration

**Answer: C.** Review before release. *(Cl. 8.6; S)*

**PS-D02.** Analysts use template version 5; version 6 is approved. What should happen?

- A) Use version 6 and withdraw version 5
- B) Use either
- C) Continue
- D) Ask the client

**Answer: A.** Current templates. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**PS-D03.** A formula in a valuation model was changed with no verification. What is affected?

- A) Context
- B) Policy
- C) Nothing
- D) Monitoring and measuring resources, and change control

**Answer: D.** Models need verification after change. *(Cl. 7.1.5, 8.5.6; T)*

**PS-D04.** A client folder is open to all staff. What is affected?

- A) Calibration
- B) Protection of client property
- C) Design
- D) Objectives

**Answer: B.** Confidential information. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**PS-D05.** An engagement used last year's rate table. What is the likely system cause?

- A) The analyst was careless
- B) Superseded data not marked obsolete and no owner for updates
- C) The client
- D) Nothing

**Answer: B.** Data control. *(Cl. 7.5.3; S)*

**PS-D06.** A review checklist item "data current" is ticked with no version noted. What is the issue?

- A) None
- B) Checklist too long
- C) Wrong pen
- D) The tick does not show the check was done

**Answer: D.** Reviews must actually verify. *(Cl. 8.6; T)*

**PS-D07.** Summary figures are typed by hand and tables change later. What is the fix?

- A) Link figures and re-review after late changes
- B) Proofread harder
- C) Shorter reports
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Method and workflow controls. *(Cl. 8.5.1; T)*

**PS-D08.** A partner sends a report before review "to save time". Which clause besides release?

- A) Calibration
- B) Context
- C) Leadership and commitment
- D) Design

**Answer: C.** Leaders support review. *(Cl. 5.1.1; S)*

**PS-D09.** A client changes scope by phone. What is required?

- A) Proceed
- B) Charge more
- C) Agree and record the change before work changes
- D) Ignore

**Answer: C.** Changes to requirements are recorded. *(Cl. 8.2.4; S)*

**PS-D10.** Original client documents are held for over 90 days with no return. What is required?

- A) A defined return process
- B) Shred them
- C) Keep them
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Client property. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**PS-D11.** Learner feedback on unclear criteria has no action. What is this?

- A) Fine
- B) A design input
- C) Not relevant
- D) Feedback not used

**Answer: D.** Feedback is analyzed and used. *(Cl. 9.1.2; S)*

**PS-D12.** A model update would change methods on ongoing work; contracts require notice. What first?

- A) Roll out
- B) Notify affected clients as their contracts require
- C) Wait a year
- D) Nothing

**Answer: B.** Customer terms. *(Cl. 8.2.3; S)*

**PS-D13.** Any analyst can overwrite the master model file. What is the risk?

- A) None
- B) Uncontrolled changes to a controlled document
- C) Speed
- D) Cost

**Answer: B.** Protect controlled documents. *(Cl. 7.5.3; T)*

**PS-D14.** A data provider delivers a corrupted dataset. What should happen?

- A) Use it
- B) Ignore
- C) Delete everything
- D) Verify inputs and reject or correct before use

**Answer: D.** External inputs are verified. *(Cl. 8.4; T)*

**PS-D15.** An assessor's competence record is current. What is this?

- A) Conforming evidence
- B) A nonconformity
- C) Not relevant
- D) A risk

**Answer: A.** Competence recorded. *(Cl. 7.2; F)*

**PS-D16.** A deliverable is marked "final" without approval. What is the issue?

- A) None
- B) A typo
- C) Status does not reflect approval
- D) Font

**Answer: C.** Status must reflect approval. *(Cl. 7.5.2; T)*

**PS-D17.** An internal email about a launch is enthusiastic. Is this a finding?

- A) Yes
- B) Yes, minor
- C) No
- D) Only if long

**Answer: C.** Not a requirement. *(Cl. 7.4; F)*

**PS-D18.** Client feedback trends are presented at management review. What does this show?

- A) Feedback is used at top level
- B) Nonconformity
- C) Nothing
- D) Too much reporting

**Answer: A.** Management review input. *(Cl. 9.3; S)*

## Part 2: Translate items

A Translate item asks you to restate a general requirement in the language of this sector, or to restate a sector example as the underlying requirement.

**PS-T01.** Which example restates "release follows planned review"?

- A) Reports are sent at 5 p.m.
- B) Reports are printed
- C) Reports are long
- D) A report goes to the client only after the peer review record is complete

**Answer: D.** Peer review is the sector form. *(Cl. 8.6; F)*

**PS-T02.** "VM-3 was edited last week with no verification record." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy
- B) Measuring resources and changes are verified
- C) Context
- D) Audit

**Answer: B.** Model control. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**PS-T03.** Which example restates "customer property is protected"?

- A) Folders are numbered
- B) Client folders are restricted to the engagement team
- C) Folders are shared
- D) Folders are printed

**Answer: B.** Confidential information. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**PS-T04.** "An assumption came from a personal spreadsheet." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Competence
- B) Objectives
- C) Context
- D) Controlled documented information is used

**Answer: D.** Use controlled sources. *(Cl. 7.5.3; T)*

**PS-T05.** Which example restates "requirements are agreed and changes recorded"?

- A) Scope changes are agreed in writing with the client before work changes
- B) Clients phone often
- C) Partners decide alone
- D) Scope is fixed forever

**Answer: A.** Requirements and changes. *(Cl. 8.2.4; S)*

**PS-T06.** "Four reports had summaries that did not match tables, closed with proofreading." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy
- B) Resources
- C) Corrective action removes the cause
- D) Context

**Answer: C.** Recurrence shows the cause remains. *(Cl. 10.2; S)*
