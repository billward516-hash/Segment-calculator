# Sector Drills: Discrete Manufacturing

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

These drills extend the discrete manufacturing sector quiz with Translate items and a scenario sort. They are untimed. In the Sector Casebook (Drills), each answer shows feedback at once and missed items return until they are secure; the pass mark for the track is 80 percent on the first try. Option order is shuffled on screen. Clause tags use 2015 numbering and are unverified. **[VERIFY]** Role tags: F frontline, T technical, S supervisor.

## Part 1: Multiple-choice items

**DM-D01.** A machinist finds drawing revision B at the workstation; the document system shows revision C. What should the machinist do?

- A) Continue, since the change is probably small
- B) Ask a colleague which revision they used
- C) Stop, obtain revision C, and inform the supervisor
- D) Finish the batch and report afterward

**Answer: C.** Only the current approved revision may be used at the point of use. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**DM-D02.** A torque wrench label shows calibration expired three weeks ago. What is correct?

- A) Do not use it; tag it and report it to the calibration owner
- B) Use it with extra care
- C) Remove the label
- D) Borrow another without checking its status

**Answer: A.** An instrument past its interval cannot be relied on. *(Cl. 7.1.5; F)*

**DM-D03.** At calibration, a micrometer is found out of tolerance. What must happen besides adjustment?

- A) Nothing more
- B) Replace the label only
- C) Delete the old results
- D) Assess the validity of earlier measurements and act on affected product

**Answer: D.** The organization reviews earlier results when an instrument is found out of tolerance. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**DM-D04.** Two batches of similar parts are mixed and their labels are lost. Which requirement is most affected?

- A) Awareness
- B) Identification and traceability
- C) Organizational context
- D) Customer satisfaction

**Answer: B.** Outputs must stay identified and, where required, traceable. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F, T)*

**DM-D05.** Bar stock arrives without the certificate required by the order. What is appropriate?

- A) Machine one part to test it
- B) Hold the lot and verify the requirement before use
- C) Accept it and ask later
- D) Judge it by appearance

**Answer: B.** External inputs are verified before use. *(Cl. 8.4; T)*

**DM-D06.** Engineering wants to raise cutting speed on a critical dimension. What comes first?

- A) The operator adjusts gradually
- B) Record it after the next audit
- C) Tell quality only
- D) Review consequences, authorize, communicate, and record the change

**Answer: D.** Production changes are reviewed and controlled. *(Cl. 8.5.6; T, S)*

**DM-D07.** Three of 50 parts fail final dimensional check. What must happen before shipping the rest?

- A) Segregate the failed parts and evaluate whether the others are affected
- B) Ship 47 with a note
- C) Re-measure only the three
- D) Rework three and ship all

**Answer: A.** Nonconforming output is controlled and release follows verification. *(Cl. 8.7, 8.6; F, S)*

**DM-D08.** Customer free-issue components arrive and one is damaged. What is correct?

- A) Use it if it looks fine
- B) Scrap it quietly
- C) Record it, protect the item, and inform the customer
- D) Replace it from own stock without telling the customer

**Answer: C.** Damaged customer property is reported to the customer. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**DM-D09.** Who may authorize release of a batch with an open inspection discrepancy?

- A) Any operator
- B) The truck driver
- C) A person with defined authority and, where applicable, the customer
- D) Nobody, ever

**Answer: C.** Release without completed arrangements needs relevant authority and, where applicable, the customer. *(Cl. 8.6; S)*

**DM-D10.** The same burr recurs and is removed by hand each time. What is missing?

- A) Investigation of cause, action to remove it, and a check that it worked
- B) Faster hand deburring
- C) A stricter final inspection only
- D) A reminder to operators

**Answer: A.** Correction deals with the output; corrective action removes the cause. *(Cl. 10.2; T)*

**DM-D11.** A machining supervisor audits his own cell. What is the concern?

- A) The audit is too short
- B) Audits must be done by customers
- C) Audits apply only to offices
- D) Objectivity and impartiality

**Answer: D.** Internal auditors must be objective and impartial. *(Cl. 9.2; S)*

**DM-D12.** A customer tightens a tolerance on an open order. What must happen?

- A) Continue unchanged
- B) Review the change, confirm capability, update documents, and inform the people affected
- C) Inform sales only
- D) Apply it to the next order

**Answer: B.** Changed requirements are reviewed and communicated. *(Cl. 8.2.4; T, S)*

**DM-D13.** A first-off inspection record shows ticks but no measured values. What is the best response as an auditor?

- A) Raise a major nonconformity at once
- B) Record an observation and ask whether values are needed for traceability and trend analysis
- C) Ignore it
- D) Ask the inspector to redo every check

**Answer: B.** Ticks show the check was done; values make it verifiable. Judge against the defined requirement. *(Cl. 8.5.1; T)*

**DM-D14.** A gauge is calibrated but uses 35 percent of the tolerance. What does this mean?

- A) It is fully suitable because it is calibrated
- B) It must be scrapped
- C) Calibration intervals must be shortened only
- D) It may not be suitable for the check even though it is accurate

**Answer: D.** Suitability for the measurement is separate from calibration. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**DM-D15.** A supplier sends a notice that it is changing an insert grade. What should happen?

- A) Review the change for its effect before accepting it, and record the decision
- B) File the notice
- C) Reject all future deliveries
- D) Let the setter decide on the day

**Answer: A.** Changes to externally provided products are evaluated before use. *(Cl. 8.4, 8.5.6; T, S)*

**DM-D16.** A shift misses three hours of in-process checks after an equipment fault. What is the system question?

- A) Who was on shift
- B) Why the operator was slow
- C) What reaction plan should apply when equipment fails, and who decides
- D) How to hide the gap

**Answer: C.** Controlled production includes defined reactions to process upsets. *(Cl. 8.5.1; S)*

**DM-D17.** A shipment target is at risk. A supervisor tells inspectors to sign release before checks are done. Which clause is most affected besides release?

- A) Calibration
- B) Infrastructure
- C) Leadership support for the quality system
- D) Design inputs

**Answer: C.** Leaders must not let targets override planned controls. *(Cl. 5.1.1; S)*

**DM-D18.** Quality objectives are posted on the shift board with this month's results. What does this show?

- A) Objectives are communicated and monitored
- B) A nonconformity because boards are informal
- C) Nothing relevant
- D) That the board needs document control

**Answer: A.** Objectives should be communicated and monitored where staff see them. *(Cl. 6.2, 7.3; F)*

## Part 2: Translate items

A Translate item asks you to restate a general requirement in the language of this sector, or to restate a sector example as the underlying requirement.

**DM-T01.** Which sector example best restates "documented information needed at the point of use must be the current version"?

- A) The quality manual is reviewed yearly
- B) The customer signs the order
- C) The canteen menu is updated weekly
- D) The setter uses the current drawing revision at the lathe and old prints are withdrawn

**Answer: D.** Point-of-use control means the right revision at the machine. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**DM-T02.** "Pallet P-31 holds parts from two batches with no separation label." Which general requirement does this restate?

- A) Competence must be recorded
- B) Outputs must stay identified so they can be traced
- C) Policy must be communicated
- D) Risks must be assessed

**Answer: B.** Mixed batches break identification. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F)*

**DM-T03.** Which sector example restates "the organization reviews the validity of earlier results when an instrument is found out of tolerance"?

- A) The gauge label is replaced
- B) After a bore gauge fails calibration, the lots it measured since its last good calibration are reviewed
- C) The gauge is moved to a new shelf
- D) A new gauge is ordered

**Answer: B.** Look-back review is the sector form of this requirement. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**DM-T04.** "A plating subcontractor changed its process without telling us." Which general requirement does this restate?

- A) Management review inputs
- B) Internal communication
- C) Organizational knowledge only
- D) Control of externally provided processes, including changes by providers

**Answer: D.** Subcontracted processes stay under the organization's control. *(Cl. 8.4; T, S)*

**DM-T05.** Which sector example restates "release of output requires completed planned verification or authorized approval"?

- A) Final inspection signs before pack-out, or a concession is authorized
- B) Packing starts when the truck arrives
- C) The operator decides when to ship
- D) The customer inspects on arrival

**Answer: A.** Release follows verification or authorization. *(Cl. 8.6; F, S)*

**DM-T06.** "CA-118 was closed with operators reminded, and burrs recurred." Which general requirement does this restate?

- A) Customer property must be protected
- B) Documented information must be retained
- C) Corrective action must remove the cause and be checked for effectiveness
- D) The scope must be defined

**Answer: C.** Recurrence shows the action did not address the cause. *(Cl. 10.2; T)*
