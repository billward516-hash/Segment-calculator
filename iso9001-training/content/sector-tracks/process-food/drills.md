# Sector Drills: Process, Food, and Consumer Goods

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

These drills extend the process, food, and consumer goods sector quiz with Translate items and a scenario sort. They are untimed. In the Sector Casebook (Drills), each answer shows feedback at once and missed items return until they are secure; the pass mark for the track is 80 percent on the first try. Option order is shuffled on screen. Clause tags use 2015 numbering and are unverified. **[VERIFY]** Role tags: F frontline, T technical, S supervisor.

## Part 1: Multiple-choice items

**PF-D01.** The label artwork on the line is the previous revision. What should happen?

- A) Finish the run, then correct
- B) Cover the difference with a sticker
- C) Stop, hold labeled product, and load the current approved artwork
- D) Let the operator decide

**Answer: C.** Obsolete documents are not used, and affected output is controlled. *(Cl. 7.5.3, 8.7; F)*

**PF-D02.** A probe used to verify processing has no verification record for the interval. What is right?

- A) Stop using it, flag it, and assess earlier readings
- B) Continue; it looked fine
- C) Ask another operator
- D) Change the limit

**Answer: A.** Measurement validity depends on verification. *(Cl. 7.1.5; F, T)*

**PF-D03.** A result needed for release is pending at end of shift. What happens?

- A) Release now and update later
- B) Release if sales asks
- C) Release on the supervisor's estimate
- D) The batch stays on hold until the result is reviewed and release authorized

**Answer: D.** Release follows planned verification. *(Cl. 8.6; F, S)*

**PF-D04.** Weighing records show "SW" with no lot code. What is affected?

- A) Policy
- B) Traceability from batch to ingredient lot
- C) Context
- D) Customer satisfaction

**Answer: B.** Without lot codes the scope of a recall is unknown. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F)*

**PF-D05.** A supplier certificate states grade B; the order requires grade A; the certificate is stamped "checked". What is wrong?

- A) Nothing
- B) The verification did not compare the certificate with the requirement
- C) The stamp colour
- D) The supplier is not approved

**Answer: B.** Verification compares inputs with requirements. *(Cl. 8.4; T)*

**PF-D06.** Customer-supplied cartons are stored outside under a sheet and one pallet is wet. What is required?

- A) Use them anyway
- B) Throw them away
- C) Dry them quietly
- D) Protect them, record the damage, and inform the customer

**Answer: D.** Customer property is protected and damage reported. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**PF-D07.** Rework from one batch goes into another without recording which cases. What is the risk?

- A) Affected product cannot be found in a recall
- B) Lower yield only
- C) Nothing
- D) Higher cost only

**Answer: A.** Rework must stay traceable. *(Cl. 8.5.2; T)*

**PF-D08.** The last mock trace was 14 months ago; the plan says every 6 months. What is this?

- A) Not relevant
- B) A customer issue
- C) A planned check not done as planned
- D) A design issue

**Answer: C.** Planned monitoring must be done. *(Cl. 9.1.1; S)*

**PF-D09.** A cooking time change is planned without trials or customer notice. What should happen?

- A) Approve for yield
- B) Try it on night shift
- C) Review the change with criteria, reviewers, and customer notice before approval
- D) Tell operators verbally

**Answer: C.** Changes are reviewed and controlled. *(Cl. 8.5.6; T, S)*

**PF-D10.** Export customers need a different date format, entered by hand monthly. What is the system weakness?

- A) A critical manual step with no defined check
- B) The customers
- C) The printer
- D) Nothing

**Answer: A.** Controlled conditions include defined methods and checks. *(Cl. 8.5.1; T)*

**PF-D11.** A recurring label error is closed with "operator retrained". What should the auditor ask?

- A) Who the operator was
- B) Why the label was printed
- C) Nothing
- D) What system condition allowed the error and how effectiveness was checked

**Answer: D.** Corrective action addresses causes. *(Cl. 10.2; S)*

**PF-D12.** Cartons are stored near the wash area at high humidity. Which requirement is most relevant?

- A) Policy
- B) Preservation
- C) Competence
- D) Design

**Answer: B.** Storage must protect materials. *(Cl. 8.5.4; F, T)*

**PF-D13.** A supervisor says "results are always fine" and wants to release early. What is the factual answer?

- A) Agree
- B) Two of the last 50 batches failed; the check exists for the one that does not pass
- C) Ignore
- D) Report the supervisor

**Answer: B.** Use data calmly. *(Cl. 8.6, 5.1.1; S)*

**PF-D14.** A customer complaint reports split cartons. Containment should cover:

- A) Only the complained delivery
- B) Only stock on site
- C) Nothing until a second complaint
- D) All batches packed in the same carton lot, including shipped product

**Answer: D.** Containment follows the suspect input. *(Cl. 8.7; S)*

**PF-D15.** A shift report notes "thin cartons" and nobody follows it up. What is the gap?

- A) Information from staff is not reviewed and acted on
- B) Nothing
- C) The shift report format
- D) The carton colour

**Answer: A.** Feedback from staff should be considered. *(Cl. 9.1.3; S)*

**PF-D16.** A label verification camera checks only the barcode. What does this mean?

- A) The camera is fine
- B) Labels are always right
- C) Other label errors, such as date format, are not detected
- D) The camera must be removed

**Answer: C.** Know what a check can and cannot detect. *(Cl. 8.6; T)*

**PF-D17.** The release record shows a hand correction without initials. What is this?

- A) A major nonconformity always
- B) Fine
- C) A weakness in record control to address
- D) Fraud

**Answer: C.** Corrections should show who made them. *(Cl. 7.5.3; T)*

**PF-D18.** A radio plays in the packing area. Is this a quality nonconformity?

- A) No, it does not affect conformity here
- B) Yes
- C) Yes, a major one
- D) Only if loud

**Answer: A.** Not every observation is a finding. *(Cl. 7.1.4; F)*

## Part 2: Translate items

A Translate item asks you to restate a general requirement in the language of this sector, or to restate a sector example as the underlying requirement.

**PF-T01.** Which example restates "outputs are identified and traceable where required"?

- A) The line is clean
- B) Operators wear uniforms
- C) The menu is posted
- D) Each case carries the batch number and the dispatch record lists batches per order

**Answer: D.** Batch identity on cases and dispatch records. *(Cl. 8.5.2; F)*

**PF-T02.** "Batch RM-504 was released at 4:20 p.m.; the result is due at 7 p.m." Which general requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy communication
- B) Release only after planned verification or authorized approval
- C) Internal audit
- D) Context

**Answer: B.** Release before verification. *(Cl. 8.6; S)*

**PF-T03.** Which example restates "the environment needed for conformity is maintained"?

- A) Staff take breaks
- B) Packaging is stored in the dry store within the humidity range set
- C) The car park is lit
- D) The radio is quiet

**Answer: B.** Environment that affects conformity. *(Cl. 7.1.4; F)*

**PF-T04.** "Recipe sheet version 7 is at the scale; version 8 changed the syrup quantity." Which general requirement does this restate?

- A) Supplier evaluation
- B) Customer feedback
- C) Objectives
- D) Current documents at the point of use

**Answer: D.** Point-of-use document control. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**PF-T05.** Which example restates "changes in externally provided products are evaluated"?

- A) A board-grade change notice from the carton supplier is reviewed before the new cartons are accepted
- B) Cartons are counted
- C) The supplier sends a calendar
- D) Invoices are paid

**Answer: A.** Supplier changes need review. *(Cl. 8.4; T)*

**PF-T06.** "Label error recurs on the first export run each month." Which general requirement does this restate?

- A) Competence
- B) Infrastructure
- C) Corrective action removes the cause and is checked for effectiveness
- D) Context

**Answer: C.** Recurrence shows the cause remains. *(Cl. 10.2; S)*
