# Sector Drills: Construction and Engineering Projects

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

These drills extend the construction and engineering projects sector quiz with Translate items and a scenario sort. They are untimed. In the Sector Casebook (Drills), each answer shows feedback at once and missed items return until they are secure; the pass mark for the track is 80 percent on the first try. Option order is shuffled on screen. Clause tags use 2015 numbering and are unverified. **[VERIFY]** Role tags: F frontline, T technical, S supervisor.

## Part 1: Multiple-choice items

**CN-D01.** A crew is using drawing revision C; revision D is current. What should happen?

- A) Continue and fix later
- B) Ask the client
- C) Stop that work and issue revision D
- D) Use both

**Answer: C.** Current drawings at the work front. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**CN-D02.** A slab is poured before the hold point is signed. What is affected?

- A) Release of work past a hold point
- B) Policy
- C) Context
- D) Calibration

**Answer: A.** Hold points must be released first. *(Cl. 8.6; F, S)*

**CN-D03.** A subcontractor starts work with no evaluation. What is right?

- A) Wait until the end
- B) Ignore it
- C) Pay them less
- D) Evaluate and approve before work continues

**Answer: D.** External providers are evaluated before use. *(Cl. 8.4; S)*

**CN-D04.** Cube press verification is overdue. What should happen?

- A) Continue
- B) Stop using it until verified and review recent results
- C) Remove the label
- D) Use another without checking

**Answer: B.** Verified test equipment. *(Cl. 7.1.5; T)*

**CN-D05.** A pour record does not show which truck went to which column. What is the risk?

- A) None
- B) Affected elements cannot be identified if a test fails
- C) Higher cost
- D) Faster pours

**Answer: B.** Traceability of work. *(Cl. 8.5.2; T)*

**CN-D06.** The driver adds water to a truck to aid placing and nobody records it. What is missing?

- A) More water
- B) Nothing
- C) A faster pump
- D) Control and record of changes during placement

**Answer: D.** Process control includes site additions. *(Cl. 8.5.1; T)*

**CN-D07.** Client-supplied fittings are stored open near a wet area. What is required?

- A) Protect them and record condition; report damage
- B) Use them anyway
- C) Return them
- D) Ignore it

**Answer: A.** Client property must be protected. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**CN-D08.** Procurement wants to switch panel suppliers to save cost. The contract requires client approval. What first?

- A) Approve now
- B) Tell the client later
- C) Complete evaluation, design review, certificates, and client approval
- D) Change only some panels

**Answer: C.** Substitutions follow review and contract terms. *(Cl. 8.2.3, 8.4; S)*

**CN-D09.** Anchor errors recur on several projects and are closed as "crew reminded". What is the auditor's question?

- A) Who the crew was
- B) Why they were slow
- C) What shared process lets steel and concrete drawings disagree
- D) Nothing

**Answer: C.** Recurring errors point to the system. *(Cl. 10.2; S)*

**CN-D10.** Water testing covered 2 of 48 windows; the ITP says 10 percent. What is this?

- A) Planned verification not completed
- B) Good practice
- C) Not relevant
- D) A design input

**Answer: A.** Do the verification as planned. *(Cl. 8.6; T)*

**CN-D11.** A foreman says "we always get it signed after". Which clause is most affected?

- A) Calibration
- B) Context
- C) Design
- D) Leadership and commitment at supervisor level

**Answer: D.** Supervisors support controls. *(Cl. 5.1.1; S)*

**CN-D12.** A certificate covers an 18 mm panel; 25 mm is specified. What is the issue?

- A) None
- B) Verification evidence does not match the product
- C) A typing error only
- D) Packaging

**Answer: B.** Certificates must match the product. *(Cl. 8.4; T)*

**CN-D13.** Changes from the client arrive by phone and are marked up later. What is the risk?

- A) None
- B) Crews may build to unrecorded or uncommunicated changes
- C) Better speed
- D) Cost only

**Answer: B.** Changes must be recorded and communicated. *(Cl. 8.2.4, 7.4; S)*

**CN-D14.** Nobody owns coordination between steel and concrete drawings. Which clause?

- A) Calibration
- B) Customer property
- C) Policy
- D) Roles, responsibilities, and authorities

**Answer: D.** Assign interface responsibility. *(Cl. 5.3; S)*

**CN-D15.** After handover the client reports leaks. What should containment include?

- A) Protection of the affected areas and checks of other projects built the same way
- B) Nothing; handover was signed
- C) Only the reported windows
- D) A discount

**Answer: A.** Containment covers similar output. *(Cl. 8.7; S)*

**CN-D16.** The cube curing tank heater failed for three days. What does this affect?

- A) Nothing
- B) Delivery times
- C) Confidence in test results
- D) Policy

**Answer: C.** Test conditions matter. *(Cl. 7.1.4; T)*

**CN-D17.** Crew competence records are current, with one faded but legible copy. Is this a finding?

- A) Yes, major
- B) Yes, minor
- C) No, the records are adequate
- D) Only if photocopied

**Answer: C.** Legible and current is adequate. *(Cl. 7.2; F)*

**CN-D18.** A weather board shows rain. Is this a quality nonconformity?

- A) No, it is planning context
- B) Yes
- C) Yes, minor
- D) Only if wrong

**Answer: A.** Not every observation is a finding. *(Cl. 7.1.4; F)*

## Part 2: Translate items

A Translate item asks you to restate a general requirement in the language of this sector, or to restate a sector example as the underlying requirement.

**CN-T01.** Which example restates "release requires completed planned arrangements or authorized approval"?

- A) The pour is booked early
- B) The crew starts at 7 a.m.
- C) The foreman approves his own work
- D) Concrete is placed only after the client engineer signs the hold point

**Answer: D.** Hold points are the sector form. *(Cl. 8.6; F)*

**CN-T02.** "The waterproofing subcontractor started with no evaluation." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Policy
- B) Control of external providers
- C) Management review
- D) Knowledge

**Answer: B.** Evaluate providers before use. *(Cl. 8.4; S)*

**CN-T03.** Which example restates "documented information is available at the point of use"?

- A) Drawings are in the office
- B) The current drawing revision is at the work front each morning
- C) The site has a printer
- D) The register is online

**Answer: B.** The work front is the point of use. *(Cl. 7.5.3; F)*

**CN-T04.** "Sealant changed from SL-9 to SL-12 mid-project, unreviewed." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Context
- B) Objectives
- C) Audit
- D) Changes by external providers are controlled

**Answer: D.** Subcontractor changes need review. *(Cl. 8.4, 8.5.6; T)*

**CN-T05.** Which example restates "customer property is protected"?

- A) Client-supplied light fittings are stored dry and their condition recorded
- B) Fittings are counted at handover
- C) Fittings are left in boxes outdoors
- D) Fittings are used early

**Answer: A.** Client property protection. *(Cl. 8.5.3; F)*

**CN-T06.** "Anchor positions differ between concrete and steel drawings by 25 mm." Which requirement does this restate?

- A) Calibration
- B) Competence
- C) Design outputs are checked for consistency
- D) Release

**Answer: C.** Design verification of interfaces. *(Cl. 8.3; T)*
