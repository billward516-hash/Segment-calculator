# Module 12: Capstone

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**Applies to:** Operators, technicians, and supervisors (required).

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module, each participant can:

1. Answer questions on the core modules with at least 80 percent accuracy.
2. Analyze a workplace scenario: identify the issue, state the immediate action, name the relevant clause, and propose a preventive action.
3. Demonstrate STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT on a mock work item.
4. Perform a document currency check.
5. Map events from the Quality Flow simulation to the clauses they relate to.

## Why it matters

The capstone confirms that participants can apply the program to real work, not only recall facts. Results are retained as evidence of competence for {{COMPANY}}.

## Capstone assessment

| Part | Time | Format | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 minutes | 20-question quiz | 80 percent or higher |
| 2 | 30 minutes | Three scenario responses, written individually | Satisfactory |
| 3 | 10 minutes | Observed demonstration | Satisfactory |

### Part 1: Knowledge quiz

Twenty questions drawn from the question bank. Operators receive the operator set. Technicians and supervisors receive role-specific sets. The pass mark is 80 percent unless the program owner sets another value.

### Part 2: Scenario responses

Respond individually to three scenarios. For each scenario, write four short answers.

1. **The issue.** What went wrong or is at risk?
2. **The immediate action.** What do you do first, and who do you tell?
3. **The clause.** Which clause (number only) relates to the situation?
4. **The preventive action.** What would stop it from happening again?

A response is satisfactory when it names the correct issue, an immediate action that contains the problem before anything else, a relevant clause, and a preventive action aimed at the cause rather than at an individual.

### Part 3: Observed demonstration

Ten minutes with an assessor and a mock work item.

1. **STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT.** Stop the process if the cause is active. Flag the work item and affected material. Hold it in a segregated location or system status. Report to the supervisor and quality immediately.
2. **Document currency check.** Show how you confirm that the instruction at your point of use is the current revision.

Satisfactory means all steps are performed in the correct order without prompting.

### Quality Flow debrief

After the Quality Flow simulation, each team maps the events it met to the clauses they relate to and states which control would have prevented each loss.

### Passing and retakes

Pass requires 80 percent on Part 1 and satisfactory results on Parts 2 and 3. A participant who does not pass receives coaching and may retake the parts not yet passed. Record the result, the assessor, the date, and any retake.
