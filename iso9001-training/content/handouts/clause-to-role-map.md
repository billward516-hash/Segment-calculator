## What each clause means for each role

| Clause | Subject | Operator | Technician | Supervisor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Context, interested parties, scope, processes | Know your process and the next step | Map and measure your process; know who depends on it | Own process maps, measures, and interfaces |
| 5 | Leadership, policy, roles | State the policy; know your authority to stop work | Apply the policy; use your authority | Hold and exercise authority; communicate policy; support reporting |
| 6 | Risks, objectives, planning of changes | Know the objective your station influences | Identify risks; follow change control | Set and monitor objectives; approve and plan changes |
| 7 | Resources, competence, awareness, communication, documents | Use current documents; record accurately; stay within your qualification | Maintain and verify instruments; keep records; train others | Ensure competence and records; manage document currency |
| 8.1 to 8.4 | Planning, customer requirements, design, external providers | Use only approved inputs | Verify inputs; review requirements; apply design controls | Review requirements and orders; manage provider performance |
| 8.5 to 8.6 | Production control, identification, traceability, customer property, preservation, release | Identify and mark status; protect customer property; follow instructions | Control process parameters; validate where needed; record traceability | Authorize release; control changes; keep release criteria under pressure |
| 8.7 | Nonconforming outputs | STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT | Contain; investigate; support disposition | Decide disposition with quality; inform customers where needed |
| 9 | Monitoring, internal audit, management review | Use station data; answer audit questions truthfully | Collect and analyze data; support audits | Review data; act on findings; contribute to management review |
| 10 | Correction, corrective action, improvement | Report problems; offer ideas | Find root causes; implement actions | Verify effectiveness; drive improvement |

## Use of this map

- Operators: concentrate on the modules marked R and the clauses in the Operator column.
- Technicians: add calibration, change control, and root cause detail.
- Supervisors: add leadership duties, objectives, internal audit, management review, competence, and risk.
