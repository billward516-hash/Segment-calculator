# Audit Response Card

**{{COMPANY}} pocket card. Clause reference: 9.2 and Module 10.**

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

## What an auditor does

An auditor gathers evidence by interview, observation, and record review. Auditors sample. Evidence counts more than statements.

## How to respond

1. **Answer the question asked.** Keep the answer short.
2. **Be truthful.** Never guess or invent an answer.
3. **Say "I will find out" when unsure.** Then tell your supervisor.
4. **Show the record or the instruction.** Evidence beats statements.
5. **Do not volunteer unrelated information.**
6. **Do not argue.** If you disagree, state your point calmly and let your supervisor follow up.
7. **Refer questions about another area** to your supervisor.

## Be ready to show

- The quality policy, and what it means for your work.
- The objectives your station influences.
- Where controlled documents are, and how you know they are current.
- How you hold nonconforming items (STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT).
- Your training record and qualification status.
- The calibration or verification status of the instruments you use.
- Records completed at the time of the activity.

## Before the audit

- Keep your area tidy and your records current.
- Remove uncontrolled copies and personal notes.
- Know your authority to stop a process.

## Audit terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Finding | A statement of what was found, compared with the criteria |
| Nonconformity | A requirement not met. Some bodies grade them as major or minor |
| Observation | A point worth improving that is not a nonconformity |

Confirm the terms used with the certification body.
