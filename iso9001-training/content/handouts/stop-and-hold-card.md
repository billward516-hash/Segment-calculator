# STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT

**{{COMPANY}} pocket card. Clause reference: 8.7.**

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

## When to use this card

Use it when you find or suspect that a work item, material, or output does not meet its requirements. This includes a result outside its limit, a damaged or mislabeled item, a missing record, or an overdue measuring resource.

## The four steps

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1. STOP** | Stop the process if the cause is still active. Do not continue work that could produce more nonconforming output. |
| **2. FLAG** | Mark the work item and any affected material. Use a physical tag or an electronic status. State what is wrong, the date, and your name. |
| **3. HOLD** | Move it to the hold area or set the hold status so that it cannot proceed or be delivered. |
| **4. REPORT** | Tell your supervisor and quality immediately. Give the work item identification, what you found, and what you did. |

## Do not

- Redo, rework, or scrap without a documented disposition.
- Release "just this once".
- Leave suspect material at the station.
- Wait to see whether the problem repeats.
- Change a record to hide the problem.

## If the item has already left

Report it at once. Quality and the supervisor decide whether the customer must be informed and what further action is needed.

## Remember

- Speed of containment protects the customer.
- Reporting is expected and valued at {{COMPANY}}.
- Only an authorized person decides what happens to a held item.
