# Module 4 Summary: Support

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: all roles (required).**

## Key points

- People, infrastructure, and the work environment are provided and maintained.
- Measuring and monitoring resources are suitable, calibrated or verified where required, identified for status, and protected. If one is found out of tolerance, earlier results are assessed.
- People are competent for their tasks, and evidence of competence is retained.
- People know the policy, their objectives, their contribution, and the effect of not conforming.
- Documented information is reviewed, approved, identified, and controlled in use, storage, change, and retention.

## Do

- Use only the current revision at the point of use.
- Check calibration or verification status before using a measuring resource.
- Record data at the time of the activity, legibly, with initials or user identification and date.
- Ask before performing a task you are not qualified for.

## Do not

- Keep personal notes or old copies that differ from the controlled instruction.
- Pre-fill or backfill a record.
- Use a resource whose calibration or verification is overdue.

## Quick check

**Question.** What do you do when a measuring resource is overdue for calibration or verification?

**Answer.** Do not use it. Flag it and report it so that earlier results can be assessed.
