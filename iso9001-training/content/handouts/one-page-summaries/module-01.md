# Module 1 Summary: Context and Scope

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: technicians and supervisors (required); operators (optional).**

## Key points

- {{COMPANY}} identifies internal and external issues that affect its ability to deliver conforming output, such as demand, technology, supply, workforce, and equipment age.
- Interested parties (customers, regulators, employees, suppliers, owners) have needs the company tracks when they are relevant.
- The scope states which products, services, sites, and activities the QMS covers, and justifies any requirement that does not apply.
- Every process has inputs, outputs, an owner, resources, measures, and risks.

## Do

- Know what your step needs from the step before it, and what the next step needs from you.
- Know who owns your process.
- Know how your step is measured.

## Do not

- Treat your step as isolated from the rest of the process.
- Run a step with no owner or no measure.

## Quick check

**Question.** Why does the scope matter at an audit?

**Answer.** It defines what the auditor may examine and states which requirements, if any, do not apply and why.
