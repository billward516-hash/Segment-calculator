# Module 6 Summary: Operation, Part B

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: all roles (required).**

## Key points

- Work is done under controlled conditions: current instructions, suitable equipment and environment, competent people, and planned checks.
- Every work item is identified, and its status (in process, on hold, released) is visible, physically or electronically.
- Customer property, physical or intangible, is identified, protected, and safeguarded. Loss or damage is reported.
- Outputs are preserved through handling, packaging, storage, and transfer.
- Release happens only when planned checks are complete, unless a person with authority, and the customer where applicable, approves otherwise.

## Do

- Keep identification and status on every work item at every step.
- Record status changes with date, time, and your identification.
- Keep each customer's material and data separate.

## Do not

- Merge or relabel work items without a record.
- Skip a check under time pressure.
- Release a work item without authority.

## Quick check

**Question.** Who may authorize release of a work item with an open discrepancy?

**Answer.** Only a person with defined release authority and, where applicable, the customer, with a record kept.
