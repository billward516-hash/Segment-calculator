# Module 5 Summary: Operation, Part A

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: technicians and supervisors (required); operators (optional).**

## Key points

- Work is planned and controlled with criteria, resources, controls, and records. Planned changes are controlled.
- Customer requirements are determined and reviewed before {{COMPANY}} commits to supply. Changed requirements are communicated to the people affected.
- Design and development is controlled where it applies. **[OWNER INPUT]** whether design is in scope.
- External providers of materials, services, and processes are evaluated and controlled in proportion to their effect, and what they supply is verified.

## Do

- Check identity, quantity, condition, version, and documents of every input at receipt.
- Confirm a verbal requirement before starting work.
- Report a supplier change notice to the supervisor.

## Do not

- Accept an order without reviewing whether it can be met.
- Use unapproved consumables, tools, or services.
- Ignore a change notice from a supplier.
