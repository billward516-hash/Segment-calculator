# Module 0 Summary: Orientation

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: all roles (required).**

## Key points

- A quality management system (QMS) is the set of processes, rules, records, and responsibilities that lets {{COMPANY}} deliver consistent, conforming output.
- ISO 9001 sets requirements for the system. Customers and the company set product and service specifications.
- Certification means an independent body has audited the system and found it conforms.
- Clauses 4 to 10 hold the auditable requirements and follow Plan, Do, Check, Act.
- Frontline staff create most of the evidence an auditor reviews.

## Do

- Know your tasks and follow the current instruction.
- Record what you did, at the time you did it.
- Report anything that does not look right.

## Do not

- Treat quality as the quality department's job only.
- Treat the system as something that exists only for audit day.

## Clause reference

Clauses 1 to 3 (background); Clauses 4 to 10 (requirements); 7.3 (awareness).

## Quick check

**Question.** Who generates most of the records in a QMS?

**Answer.** Frontline staff, because they record the work as it happens.
