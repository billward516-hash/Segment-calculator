# Module 8 Summary: Performance Evaluation

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: technicians and supervisors (required); operators (optional).**

## Key points

- {{COMPANY}} decides what to monitor and measure, how, and when to analyze the results.
- Customer perception is monitored through complaints, surveys, scorecards, and feedback.
- Internal audits are planned, impartial, and reported to management. Findings are corrected without undue delay.
- Management review at planned intervals considers performance, audit results, risks, resources, and improvement, and produces decisions and actions.

## Do

- Keep station charts and boards current and use them in team discussions.
- Help internal auditors by showing records and explaining your work.
- Follow up actions assigned to you until they are closed.

## Do not

- Collect data that nobody reviews.
- Treat internal audit as a formality.
- Hold a review that ends without actions, owners, and dates.

## Clause reference

9.1, 9.2, 9.3

## Quick check

**Question.** Why must internal auditors be impartial?

**Answer.** An auditor cannot fairly judge their own work. Impartiality keeps the results credible.
