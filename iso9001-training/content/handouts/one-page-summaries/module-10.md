# Module 10 Summary: Audits: What to Expect

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: all roles (required).**

## Key points

- Audit types: internal, customer, certification, surveillance, and recertification.
- An audit runs through an opening meeting, evidence gathering, findings, a closing meeting, corrective action, and follow-up.
- Auditors sample. Evidence carries more weight than statements.
- Finding terms vary by certification body: major nonconformity, minor nonconformity, observation or opportunity for improvement. **[VERIFY]**

## Do

- Answer the question asked, truthfully.
- Show the record or the instruction.
- Say "I will find out" when unsure, and tell your supervisor.
- Refer questions about another area to your supervisor.

## Do not

- Guess.
- Volunteer unrelated information.
- Argue about a finding on the spot.
- Hide, tidy, or alter records.

## Clause reference

9.2 (internal audit); certification audit practice is set by the certification body.

## Quick check

**Question.** Why is a record stronger evidence than a statement?

**Answer.** A record was made at the time of the work and can be checked by someone else. A statement depends on memory.
