# Module 12 Summary: Capstone

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

**{{COMPANY}} one-page summary. Applies to: all roles (required).**

## Key points

- The capstone checks that you can apply the program to real work.
- **Part 1 (30 minutes):** 20-question quiz. Pass at 80 percent.
- **Part 2 (30 minutes):** three written scenario responses: the issue, the immediate action, the clause, the preventive action.
- **Part 3 (10 minutes):** observed demonstration of STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT with a mock work item, and a document currency check.
- A participant who does not pass receives coaching and retakes the parts not yet passed. Results go on the training record.

## Do

- Contain the problem first in every scenario.
- Aim the preventive action at the cause, not at a person.
- Perform the demonstration steps in order without prompting.

## Do not

- Start with re-measuring, rework, or investigation before containment.
- Name "retrain the operator" as the only preventive action.

## Clause reference

4 to 10 (all clauses).

## Quick check

**Question.** In a scenario response, what comes first: the investigation or the immediate action?

**Answer.** The immediate action that contains the problem. Investigation follows.
