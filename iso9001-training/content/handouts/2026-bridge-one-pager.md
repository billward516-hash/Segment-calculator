## What it means for you

| Role | What it means | What to do now |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | Record true values, report problems and near-misses promptly, and use your stop-work authority | Know the reporting channel; use the STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT card |
| Technician | Record actual readings, escalate excursions, share know-how, and name opportunities as well as risks | Add lessons learned after each fix; suggest one improvement |
| Supervisor | Thank people who raise concerns, hold release criteria under pressure, plan changes with communication and review | Use the Change Planning Record; add opportunities to the risk register; complete the gap assessment |
| Top management | Promote and demonstrate quality culture and ethical behavior, with evidence | Add culture and conduct to management review; decide transition timing |
