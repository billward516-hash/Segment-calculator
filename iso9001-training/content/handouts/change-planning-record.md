# Change Planning Record

**{{COMPANY}} template. Clause reference: 6.3 (2015 and 2026), linked to 8.5.6.**

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

The 2026 edition is reported to strengthen the planning of QMS changes with communication, monitoring, evaluation, and review (6.3, High, unverified). The 2015 edition already requires planned changes (6.3) and control of changes in operations (8.5.6). Fields 6 to 8 reflect the reported 2026 emphasis. Using them now is good practice under either edition.
