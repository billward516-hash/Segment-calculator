# Troubleshooting

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

This guide gives instructors of the {{COMPANY}} ISO 9001 training program a response to the problems that most often arise in a session. For each problem, act early, stay calm, and keep the session on time.

## 1. Participation

| Problem | Signs | Response |
|---|---|---|
| One participant dominates | Answers every question; interrupts others | Use timed turns: each person has 30 seconds. Assign role cards (speaker, recorder, timekeeper, challenger) and rotate them. Thank the participant and ask, "Who has not spoken yet?" |
| Participants disengage | Silence, phones, side conversations | Switch to a table activity. Give each person a role card. Ask a direct, concrete question about their own work rather than a general one. |
| A quiet participant has a good point | Writes notes but does not speak | Invite written answers on cards, then read them out without names. |
| Side conversations | Two or more people talking while others speak | Pause and ask the table to share its point with the room. |
| Participants with limited literacy or language | Difficulty with handouts or quizzes | Read questions aloud. Use pictures, demonstrations, and the hold tag. Offer an oral knowledge check with an assessor. **[OWNER INPUT]** confirm that oral assessment is accepted. |

## 2. Resistance

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already know this." | Move to scenario cards or the Workplace Evidence Hunt. Let the evidence show the gaps. Ask the group to teach one point to the room. |
| "This is just for the auditors." | Ask for an example of a problem that reached a customer. Link it to the control that would have stopped it. |
| "Quality is the quality department's job." | Ask who touched the last work item before it shipped. Point to Module 0 and the role matrix. |
| "We do not have time to follow the procedure." | Acknowledge the pressure. Ask what happens when an item is reworked or returned. Record the concern and pass it to the supervisor as a possible improvement. |
| "Reporting gets people in trouble." | State the company's non-punitive reporting stance. **[OWNER INPUT]** Do not promise outcomes you cannot guarantee. Refer the concern to the program owner. |
| Challenge to the instructor's knowledge of the standard | Thank the participant. Check the point against the standard after the session and report back. Do not guess. |

## 3. Time

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Running behind schedule | Use the time-saving options in the module facilitation notes. Drop optional modules for the role. Keep STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT, the document currency check, and the measuring resource status check in every format. |
| Late start | Shorten the opening and the first break. Do not shorten the assessment. |
| Discussion runs long | Record open points on a "parking lot" sheet and answer them at the end or in writing. |
| Ahead of schedule | Use the extension options in the module facilitation notes, or run a Clause Sprint round. |

## 4. Games and technology

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Digital game fails to load or freezes | Switch to the printable version, which uses the same data. Keep the printables for the chosen industry pack in the room for every session. |
| Game progress lost after a refresh | Accept the offer to resume. If none appears, restart with the same seed and enter the round reached. |
| Projector fails | Run the game from the printed facilitator key and a flip chart scoreboard. |
| Download of the game log is blocked | Copy the log text from the box on the screen. |
| Teams finish rounds at different speeds | Give the fast team the next event card face down and a debrief question to discuss while waiting. |
| A team falls far behind on score | Remind the group that the debrief, not the score, is the goal. Ask the team what it would buy on a replay. |
| Game rules disputed | The facilitator decides on the spot. Record the point for the program owner. |

## 5. Sensitive situations

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Disclosure of a real incident | Pause. Acknowledge the person. Do not discuss names, blame, or details in the room. Take the discussion offline with quality after the session. |
| Disclosure of a possible current nonconformity | Thank the person. After the session, report it to the supervisor and quality so that it is assessed under the normal process. |
| Disclosure of a safety, legal, or ethical concern | Follow {{COMPANY}} reporting rules. **[OWNER INPUT]** reporting route. |
| Criticism of a named person | Redirect to the process: "What in the system allowed that?" |
| Conflict between participants | Pause the activity. Restate the ground rules. Speak to the individuals at the break. |

## 6. Assessment

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Participant fails the knowledge check | Review the missed topics. Coach. Arrange a retake with a new set of questions. Record both attempts. |
| Participant is anxious about the assessment | Explain the format, the pass mark, and the retake process before starting. |
| Suspected copying | Separate the participants. Offer a new question set under supervision. |
| Practical check cannot be done on the floor | Use a mock work item in the classroom. Note the location on the record. |
| Participant absent for part of the course | Record the modules completed. Schedule the missing modules before assessment. |

## 7. Content questions

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Question about the 2026 edition | Point to Module 11 and its confidence labels. Do not add unverified detail. Refer timing questions to the quality lead. |
| Question about a sector scheme that builds on ISO 9001 | State that this program covers ISO 9001 only. Refer the question to quality. |
| Question about a company procedure the instructor does not know | Say, "I will find out," record it, and answer in writing after the session. |
| Participant quotes a requirement differently from the guide | Thank the participant. The guide paraphrases and is not the standard. Check the purchased text and report back. |
