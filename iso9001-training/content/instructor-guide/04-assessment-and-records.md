## 2. Knowledge check

- **Source.** Twenty questions drawn from the question bank (`data/questions.json`) through the quiz tool or the printed quiz sheets.
- **Sets.** Operators receive the operator subset. Technicians and supervisors receive role-specific sets.
- **Time.** 30 minutes.
- **Conditions.** Individual work. Participant guide closed. Handouts closed.
- **Pass mark.** 80 percent (16 of 20). **[OWNER INPUT]** confirm or change.
- **Feedback.** Review each incorrect answer with the participant, using the rationale in the question bank.
- **Retake.** After coaching on the missed topics, the participant takes a new set of 20 questions. A different set is drawn for each attempt.

## 4. Capstone assessment (Format C)

| Part | Time | Format | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 minutes | 20-question quiz | 80 percent or higher |
| 2 | 30 minutes | Three scenario responses, written individually: the issue, the immediate action, the clause, the preventive action | Satisfactory |
| 3 | 10 minutes | Observed demonstration of STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT with a mock work item, and a document currency check | Satisfactory |

**Part 2 marking.** A response is satisfactory when it names the correct issue, gives an immediate action that contains the problem first, cites a relevant clause, and proposes a preventive action aimed at the cause rather than at a person. Two of three scenarios must be satisfactory, and no response may release a nonconforming work item without authority. **[OWNER INPUT]** confirm the two-of-three rule.

**Part 3 marking.** Use Parts A and B of the practical check observation checklist.

**Pass.** 80 percent on Part 1 and satisfactory on Parts 2 and 3. A participant who does not pass receives coaching and retakes only the parts not yet passed.
