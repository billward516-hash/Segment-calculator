# 2026 Bridge Session

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

This chapter gives scripts, timing, and answer keys for the three bridging sessions in Appendix A.7. It is for staff who have completed the core 2015 program. Every statement about the 2026 edition carries a confidence label. Show the labels to participants. Paraphrase only; never read requirement wording aloud as a quotation. Do not cite lettered sub-items within Clause 5.

> **Confidence labels.** High: three or more sources agree. Medium: two sources, or partial agreement. Single: one source. Unclear: sources differ or give no detail. Best estimate: inferred by the author where sources are silent or disagree, with the reasoning and what to check stated.

### 3.1 Script

**0:00 What stays the same.** "Clauses 4 to 10, the process approach, and risk-based thinking stay (High). Clause 8 controls such as traceability, release, and nonconforming output are reported as carried forward in intent, but sources differ on wording (Unclear). Internal audit, management review, and corrective action: no substantive change reported (Medium)."

**0:05 Culture and ethics.** Explain the reported 5.1.1 and 7.3 changes (High) and the 7.1.4 note (Medium). Play CU01 in groups of four. Each role chooses A, B, or C in secret. Reveal and read consequences. Ask: "Which conditions made speaking up easy or hard? What can leaders change?" Then play CU02 if time allows.

**0:20 Risks and opportunities.** "Risks could stop us reaching our results. Opportunities could improve them. The 2026 edition is reported to address them in separate subclauses, 6.1.1 to 6.1.3 (High; numbering unverified)." Show the worked example. Ask each participant to name one risk and one opportunity at their station. Use S104 (risks only) and S110 (conforming) to show the difference.

**0:35 Planning of changes.** "Changes are planned, communicated, monitored, and reviewed (6.3, High)." Walk through the nine fields of the Change Planning Record. Fields 6 to 8 (communication, monitoring, review) are the reported additions. Apply them to S105.

**0:45 Bridge Sprint.** Read each statement. Teams hold up *Unchanged*, *New or changed in 2026*, or *Not sure; verify*. Reveal the answer, name the clause in both editions, and read the confidence label.

> Until verification, the verify card earns 1 point on Single, Unclear, and Best estimate items.

**0:55 Actions.** Each supervisor writes one action for their area on the gap assessment worksheet. Collect the evaluation.

## 5. Bridge games

**Culture Under Pressure** (30 to 40 minutes; Modules 2, 4, 7). Groups of four: operator, technician, supervisor, manager. Each role chooses one of three responses in secret. Reveal, read the consequence of each path, and score. Each role choice earns 0, 1 or 2 points. A supervisor or manager response that supports the person who raised the concern earns 1 extra point. Debrief: which conditions made speaking up easy or hard, and what can leaders change?

**Safety rules.** Fictional scenarios only. No real names or incidents. The facilitator stops any discussion that turns to real individuals. Ethics content is reviewed by Human Resources or Legal before delivery; no disciplinary or legal guidance is given in this game.

**Bridge Sprint** (10 minutes). A variant of Clause Sprint using 24 statements. Once Appendix A is verified, remove the partial credit and update the answers.

> **Scoring.** Matching card 2 points. For Single, Unclear and Best estimate items, the verify card also earns 1 point.
