# Course Formats and Agendas

*This material is an original training aid. It is not a substitute for the standard. Obtain ISO 9001 from ISO or a national standards body.*

This guide sets out the five delivery formats of the {{COMPANY}} ISO 9001 training program and gives a full agenda for each. Times are elapsed times from the start of the session. Clause numbers follow ISO 9001:2015. Items marked **[OWNER INPUT]** need company information.

## 1. Choosing a format

| Format | Audience | Duration | Modules | Games |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Operator Essentials** | Operators and other frontline staff | 3 hours | 0, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10 (condensed) | Quality Flow short form or Workplace Evidence Hunt; Clause Sprint warm-up |
| **B. Technician and Supervisor Core** | Technicians and supervisors | 6 hours (one day) | 0 to 10 | Document Control Relay, Root Cause Relay, Quality Flow (full) |
| **C. Full Program** | Supervisors and quality champions | 2 days (about 14 hours) | 0 to 12 | Three or more, including Audit Day and Management Review Boardroom |
| **D. Micro-sessions** | All roles | 15 to 20 minutes each | One topic per session | Clause Sprint (5-minute round), Workplace Evidence Hunt (single item), exit ticket |
| **E. Sector Deep-Dive Track** | Anyone who has completed general instruction or passed a placement check | 3 to 4 hours per sector | Sector primer, sector drills, six cases, capstone | Sector Casebook (Case Room) |

**Selection rules.**

1. Every participant completes the modules marked required for the role in the role matrix of the participant guide.
2. Operators take Format A. Technicians and supervisors take Format B. Quality champions and supervisors with audit or management review duties take Format C.
3. Format D reinforces any of Formats A to C. It does not replace them for new starters.
4. Format E is optional and follows general instruction.
5. Mixed groups follow the format of the most senior role present. Give operators the optional modules as listening only and assess them on the operator set.

**Game materials.** Every game named in these agendas is included. Digital: Quality Flow (`games/quality-flow/`), Quiz and Clause Sprint (`games/quiz/`), Audit Day (`games/audit-day/`), Sector Casebook (`games/casebook/`), and the 2026 bridge games (`games/bridge/`). Printed kits for Root Cause Relay, Document Control Relay, Traceability Challenge, Management Review Boardroom, Workplace Evidence Hunt, and Change Control Challenge are in `games/printables/`. Facilitation for each game is in the game facilitation chapter.

## 2. Format A: Operator Essentials (3 hours)

**Objectives.** By the end, participants can state what the policy means at their station, check that an instruction is current, check calibration status before use, identify and mark work items, apply STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT, and answer an auditor truthfully.

**Materials.** Participant guide Modules 0, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, and 10; STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT card; audit response card; Quality Flow (6 rounds) or the Workplace Evidence Hunt checklist; blank hold tags; evaluation forms; sign-in sheet.

| Time | Activity | Module | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Welcome, objectives, why the QMS matters | 0 | Opening question, short talk, Clause Sprint warm-up (one round) |
| 0:15 to 0:40 | Policy, objectives, authority | 2 | Policy in own words, stop-work authority discussion |
| 0:40 to 1:10 | Documents, calibration, competence | 4 | Revision check demonstration, calibration label examples |
| 1:10 to 1:20 | Break | | |
| 1:20 to 1:50 | Production control, traceability, customer property | 6 | Status marking examples, trace a work item |
| 1:50 to 2:10 | Nonconforming outputs: STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT, with correction versus corrective action | 7, 9 (condensed) | Card walk-through, practice with a hold tag; last 5 minutes on correction versus corrective action |
| 2:10 to 2:50 | Game: Quality Flow short form (6 rounds) or Workplace Evidence Hunt | 4, 6, 7 | Teams; clause-mapping debrief in the last 10 minutes |
| 2:50 to 3:00 | Audit response card, quick check, evaluation | 10 (condensed) | Read the card aloud, three quick check questions, evaluation form |

**Condensing Modules 9 and 10.** For Module 9, teach one point only: a correction fixes the output, and a corrective action removes the cause. For Module 10, read the response guidelines on the audit response card and practice one question: "Show me the instruction you are using."

**Assessment.** Operator knowledge check (20 questions) at the end of the session or within one week. Practical check on the floor within two weeks (see the assessment guide).

## 3. Format B: Technician and Supervisor Core (6 hours)

**Objectives.** By the end, participants can do everything in Format A and can also explain context and scope, risk-based thinking, planned change, calibration and competence requirements, requirement review, external provider control, root-cause analysis, internal audit, and management review.

**Materials.** Participant guide Modules 0 to 10; all three handouts; Document Control Relay; Root Cause Relay; Quality Flow (12 rounds); evaluation forms; sign-in sheet.

| Time | Activity | Module | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:20 | Orientation | 0 | Opening question, structure of the standard, Plan, Do, Check, Act |
| 0:20 to 1:00 | Context, scope, leadership | 1, 2 | Process map of own work (15 minutes), policy and authority discussion |
| 1:00 to 1:40 | Planning, risk, change | 3 | Risk and control pairs at own station, change scenario |
| 1:40 to 1:50 | Break | | |
| 1:50 to 2:40 | Support with Document Control Relay | 4 | Short talk (20 minutes), Relay (25 minutes), debrief (5 minutes) |
| 2:40 to 3:30 | Operation A and B | 5, 6 | Requirement review example, receiving verification, status and traceability |
| 3:30 to 4:15 | Lunch | | |
| 4:15 to 4:45 | Nonconforming outputs and improvement with Root Cause Relay | 7, 9 | STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT (10 minutes), Relay (20 minutes) |
| 4:45 to 5:30 | Quality Flow (full, 12 rounds) | 3 to 10 | Teams; facilitator view on the projector |
| 5:30 to 6:00 | Debrief, audit expectations, assessment | 8, 10 | Clause-mapping debrief (10 minutes), Modules 8 and 10 key points (10 minutes), quick check and evaluation (10 minutes) |

**Notes.** Modules 7 and 9 share 30 minutes. Keep STOP, FLAG, HOLD, REPORT intact and shorten the Relay if needed. Modules 8 and 10 are taught through the Quality Flow debrief and the closing slot. Supervisors who need more depth on internal audit and management review should take Format C.

**Assessment.** Role-specific knowledge check (20 questions) and practical check, as set out in the assessment guide.

**Materials.** Everything for Format B, plus the Traceability Challenge, Audit Day dossier, Management Review Boardroom data packet, Module 11 and Module 12, capstone question sets, scenario sheets, and a mock work item for the observed demonstration.

## 5. Format D: Micro-sessions (15 to 20 minutes)

**Purpose.** Reinforce one behavior at a time, close to the work, at shift start or in a team meeting.

**Structure of every session.** One topic, one example, one question, one action.

| Time | Step | What the facilitator does |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:02 | Topic | State the topic and why it matters at this workplace. |
| 0:02 to 0:07 | Example | Present one example, fictional or real with permission. |
| 0:07 to 0:12 | Question | Ask one question. Take two or three answers. Confirm the correct answer. |
| 0:12 to 0:15 | Action | Name one action each person will take on this shift. |
| 0:15 to 0:20 | Optional | Clause Sprint 5-minute round, single-item Workplace Evidence Hunt, or exit ticket question. |

**Schedule.** Run one session per week for 24 weeks, following the series in the micro-session guide. Sessions may be repeated for each shift. A session may be moved to follow a relevant event, such as an audit finding or a complaint.

**Facilitator.** A supervisor or team leader who has completed Format B, or a quality representative.

**Records.** Record date, topic number, facilitator, and attendees on the sign-in sheet. Micro-sessions are awareness activities. They do not by themselves establish competence for a task. **[OWNER INPUT]** whether attendance is entered in the training system.

## 6. Format E: Sector Deep-Dive Track (3 to 4 hours per sector)

**Entry.** Completion of Format A, B, or C, or a pass on a placement check drawn from the general question bank.

**Content.** A sector primer, sector drills (pass mark 80 percent), six investigation cases in the Sector Casebook, a sector capstone, and a reflection. The track has no hard locks. A learner may repeat cases and may take further sectors.

**Modes.** Self-paced, or instructor-led as Case Room sessions. Other classroom modes are pairs, individual play, jigsaw (different teams take different case types), and cross-sector comparison.

**Availability.** Sector primers and drills are in the Sector Deep-Dive Track book (`sector-tracks.html`). The Sector Casebook is at `games/casebook/index.html`, with the results reader for instructors at `games/casebook/results-reader.html`.

### Instructor-led agenda (4 hours)

| Time | Activity | Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Introduction, placement check if needed | State the sector, the badge criteria, and how results are recorded |
| 0:15 to 0:45 | Sector primer | Group reading and discussion of the sector's terms, outputs, and typical controls |
| 0:45 to 1:15 | Sector drills | Individual, untimed, with immediate feedback; repeat until secure |
| 1:15 to 1:25 | Break | |
| 1:25 to 2:25 | Case Room session (one case type, same case code for all) | See the session plan below |
| 2:25 to 2:35 | Break | |
| 2:35 to 3:20 | Jigsaw | Teams each take one of the remaining case types |
| 3:20 to 3:40 | Jigsaw report-back | Each team presents the issue, the evidence, the clause, and the best action |
| 3:40 to 4:00 | Reflection and next steps | One change to make at work; plan the capstone and instructor review |

### Case Room session plan (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:05 | Introduce the sector and case type; give the case code |
| 0:05 to 0:30 | Learners investigate individually or in pairs |
| 0:30 to 0:45 | Compare findings; reveal the coverage map |
| 0:45 to 0:55 | Discuss missed evidence, false findings, and the best actions |
| 0:55 to 1:00 | Reflection: one change to make at work |

**Facilitator prompts.** What did you notice first? What made you look at that record? Which evidence looked suspicious but was conforming? What system condition allowed the issue? What would you check at your own workplace?

**Badge review.** The Sector Practitioner badge requires an instructor to review at least the capstone findings and, preferably, to observe a short oral or on-the-job demonstration. No score decides the badge on its own. Record name, role, sector, cases completed, dates, instructor, and outcome. **[OWNER INPUT]** system of record and retention period.
